# Unconfirmed

Open questions: what was not traced or confirmed in the game, and what the viewer's model does not explain yet.
Open items about puzzle-only mechanics, which the viewer's walk does not use, are in [Out of scope](#out-of-scope).

## The game's mechanics

- **The first drift of a Lily clone.** A Lily that a Spawner respawns on a river appears at the Spawner's cell and drifts ([Activators and Spawners](MOVEMENT.md#activators-and-spawners)). The entity clone routine's copy of the prototype's fields was not read in full. The Spawner sets no direction, so the first step follows the ordinary Lily rule. The game has one such Lily at a time, but the walk counts the drift from every Spawner together ([Skip lanes and the TELEPORT swap](VIEWER.md#skip-lanes-and-the-teleport-swap)).

- **PROTECTION without a hit.** `protected` is set only when an attack or fire hits a `pre_protected` Guy ([Goblins, dragons and PROTECTION](MOVEMENT.md#goblins-dragons-and-protection)), yet in recorded play a Guy pulled onto open water beside a PROTECTION Guy is held at the surface where no attack or fire reaches him, and so is a Guy over water who did not move that turn (`both_ways`, moves 48 and 54, counting from 0). The game's code gives one way for this: a hit protects the victim's whole turn group and that of every PROTECTION Guy beside him, and a group member away from every PROTECTION Guy keeps `protected` until a later protection pass finds him `pre_protected` ([Goblins, dragons and PROTECTION](MOVEMENT.md#goblins-dragons-and-protection)). Not checked: whether these Guys share a turn group with a Guy hit earlier.
- **A goblin's attack across a Fence.** The attack takes the move check's fence test, so it does not cross a blocking Fence ([Goblins, dragons and PROTECTION](MOVEMENT.md#goblins-dragons-and-protection)). In recorded play a PROTECTION Guy beside a goblin across such a Fence is still protected and does not fall (`flamestrike_v3`, moves 42–44). Most likely a `protected` left over from an earlier hit on his turn group, as in the entry above; not checked.
- **A monster Gate open beside a living goblin.** The update's room and Monster rules are checked in the game's code ([Gates, switches and buttons](MOVEMENT.md#gates-switches-and-buttons)), yet recorded play shows one open while a living goblin stands in the same screen (`open_monster`, move 78). Not checked: whether the goblin falls in another layer band than the Gate, fails the existence test, or is counted from its cell before the move.
- **A dragon's line of Monsters.** By the game's code a kill gets its own settle step, so one dragon kills every Monster along its line in one input, one per step ([Goblins, dragons and PROTECTION](MOVEMENT.md#goblins-dragons-and-protection)). Recorded play shows one kill per dragon per input (`heroes2_11_v2`, `fuse`, `surrounded`). Not checked: what keeps the follow-up settle from tracing the fire again.
- **A magic-Mirror arrival under water.** The teleport's finish runs the drown check, so a Guy who lands 1 or 2 cells under a water surface drowns at once ([Magic mirrors](MOVEMENT.md#magic-mirrors)), yet in recorded play such a Guy is still there after the move (`red_cross` move 17, `mirror_10_d` move 16, counting from 0). Not checked: the landing height the game picks over water, and whether the recording was taken before the death.
- **A Fence in a Group that exists by default.** In recorded play the hero steps through a blocking Fence on his first move (`heroes1_1`), though the Fence's existence Group exists by default ([Existence groups](ENTITIES.md#existence-groups)). The game's code gives such a Fence no exemption: a Group member counts as existing until its Group's status is applied, the level load applies `exists_by_default`, and the fence test takes every Fence that exists. Leads: the Fence's `blocking` or its edge in the level data, or a first move made during the prelude.

## Out of scope

- Whether a second SMASH move on one Rock in a charm move changes the outcome was not traced ([CHARM](SKILLS.md#charm)).
- The Torc's exact rule was not traced ([TRANSMUTE and the other skills](SKILLS.md#transmute-and-the-other-skills)).
