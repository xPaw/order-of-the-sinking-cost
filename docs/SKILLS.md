# Moves and skills

The hero's inputs: skills, wardrobes, a direction press, TELEPORT and PULL, CHARM, TRANSMUTE and the other skills,
winning, and the move flags.

`Guy.skill_flags` (`Skill_Flags`): MULTIPUSH 1, SINGLE_PUSH 2, PULL 4, TELEPORT 8, PROTECTION 16, TRANSMUTE 32, CHARM 64,
TAKE_GEMS 128, QUEEN 256, CHARM_VISUALLY_3D 512, SAILOR 1024, TRADER 2048. Each skill is an independent bit test.
`inventory_flags` (`Inventory_Flags`) hold the Promesst colours (bit 1 << `Promesst_Power`, bits 0–9) and items (WAND 0x400, TORC 0x800, PRISM 0x1000). The
overworld Guy's data `skill_flags` hold no PROTECTION.

## Wardrobes

- A Wardrobe's cell is walkable from every side. Only its door, the edge its +x axis points to, does anything. Other edges and vertical moves change nothing.
- At most one Wardrobe applies per move, the one being left first: in the source cell, the move's component along the door's facing (+x) above 0.1 is door-out; else in the target cell, below −0.1 is door-in. A step is one cell along an axis, so the threshold only tells the door's edge from the other three.
- Door-out: `skill_flags = (skill_flags & ~SINGLE_PUSH) | Wardrobe.skill_flags`. An inventory-only Wardrobe adds its `inventory_flags` instead.
- Door-in: a skill Wardrobe resets the hero to QUEEN | SINGLE_PUSH (258), which drops CHARM and every other skill. An inventory Wardrobe clears `inventory_flags`, and a TAKE_GEMS hero's carried Gems are destroyed. The door-in priority chain only picks a sound.
- So skills stack: leave one door, enter a second Wardrobe through its back or side, leave its door holding both.
- SINGLE_PUSH comes back only through: a door-in reset; the INTRO return (teleporting the hero to a link Marker in a level whose `special_level` is INTRO runs every Wardrobe's door-in reset); un-transmuting (the crystal Rock stores the Guy's skill and inventory and restores them, inventory minus bit 0x20000); a Spawner-made Guy (`Spawner.skill_flags | QUEEN`); the SAILOR Wardrobes, whose skills include SINGLE_PUSH.
- On the overworld only Wardrobe doors change the skill. Teleporter pads, leaving a level, the wizard swap and the hero swap keep it. Undo and load restore it like any saved field.
- Fire does not cross a Wardrobe door edge.

## A direction press

The game resolves a press in this order:

1. Beam powers shape the move: DOUBLE multiplies it, ENDPOINT turns a move along the beam into a jump ([Promesst beams and powers](MOVEMENT.md#promesst-beams-and-powers)).
2. A hero inside a solid voxel, a Tree's cell or a frozen Freezer's cell, outside a THROUGH beam, only turns.
3. A hero with TELEPORT swaps if his line finds a target ([TELEPORT and PULL](#teleport-and-pull)).
4. An ENDPOINT jump happens if the landing passes the move check; otherwise the hero bumps.
5. A hero with CHARM makes a charm move ([CHARM](#charm)).
6. Otherwise the hero steps: the move check (blockers, recursive pushes, the Friendly_Dragon swap), then the water and ledge check ([Water](MOVEMENT.md#water), [Falling](MOVEMENT.md#falling)), then the push-strength check ([Pushing and pulling](MOVEMENT.md#pushing-and-pulling)). Then the moves are added, then PULL ([TELEPORT and PULL](#teleport-and-pull)).
7. A failed step is a bump: a STATIONARY move that costs the turn and turns the hero. Each unmoved entry of its push list gets the SMASH or OPEN effect of its pusher ([Pushing and pulling](MOVEMENT.md#pushing-and-pulling)). With TRANSMUTE he then transmutes what he bumped.

The move check's SMASH test runs only in move checks that don't collect pushes or have pushing off: the jump of step
4 and the charm move of step 5. A step pushes a Rock or Rover ahead, and a SMASH hero smashes only when the push fails,
through the bump over the push list of step 7 ([Promesst beams and powers](MOVEMENT.md#promesst-beams-and-powers)). A step that would push while the request's "no push" byte is set becomes a "no_multipush" bump;
player input always sets it to 0. A Gem is not a blocker for a TAKE_GEMS Guy.

Presses while busy:

- While the active hero's current transaction is a falling one, his queued press waits at the head of the queue and runs when the fall ends.
- An ordinary fall step never counts against the press, so a press survives an ordinary fall of any length and runs when he lands. The count goes up only when beam powers turn one of his fall steps into a TELEPORT move that passes the move check: a DOUBLE fall step (×2 or ×3), or the ENDPOINT jump down a vertical beam that follows the first STATIONARY pause (`stationary_endpoint_fall_duration`, TELEPORT_FALL_STATIONARY_MOVE). A step that vanishes below the Annihilator does not count. On the 3rd such step all his queued presses are dropped.
- A skip does not hold the player. Its transaction carries only IS_SKIPPING_TRANSACTION; it gets BLOCKING_PLAYER only when a move in it has DIE_BY_ROASTING or DIE_BY_GOBLIN_ATTACK. The hero's push marks the stone's move as a launch (lasting `time_per_skip`) and raises a skip request. One level update then runs, in order: the transaction pass finishes moves, and when the launch ends the Rock becomes SKIPPING and the request is raised again; the skip driver runs when the request is set and no skipping transaction is live, or when one finishes (chaining with the leftover time); the queued input runs last, unless a live transaction blocks the player. So the first skip starts in the update that finishes the hero's step, before the input queue, and a press made during the skip runs while the stone is SKIPPING.
- If his group has a TELEPORT Guy, the press runs at once, so the wizard can swap mid-fall. A Marker with `script_name` `wait_for_fall` makes a TELEPORT hero standing in its box wait while its `script_target_entity_id` is FALLING above him.

## TELEPORT and PULL

TELEPORT (Wizard) has no separate input. The swap is the move.

- The line runs from the hero's cell along the move vector only (the move vector after the beam powers), at his z, one move step at a time: every 2nd or 3^n-th cell in DOUBLE beams. It never looks backwards and has no special case for another hero. It is the dragon-fire tracer.
- Targets: a Guy, Rock, Gem, Promesst_Light, Monster, Friendly_Dragon or Rover that is not DEAD, at any distance, and a Mirror the line meets on its back or a closed side. The tracer returns every hit in the first cell that has one, in cell-list order (insertion order: appended on insert, swapped with the last on removal). There is no type priority, and every hit swaps: the wizard goes to that cell, and each hit gets a TELEPORT move to his old cell ("SwapTargetPerson", "SwapTargetRock"). So a Guy and the Gem he stands on both come to the wizard's cell. The line skips an entity with a pending move flagged DIE_BY_ROASTING or DIE_BY_GOBLIN_ATTACK.
- The hero lands exactly where the target was and the target where he was. No water check and no move check; each then falls, sinks or drowns by the normal rules. A Rock swapped into water sinks to surface − 1.
- The swap is one transaction: the hero and every hit get a TELEPORT move to each other's cell, all with the same duration. Each moved entity settles after the move, so a swapped object that lands over nothing falls, with the usual water rules ([Falling](MOVEMENT.md#falling), [Objects in water](MOVEMENT.md#objects-in-water)). A Switch takes its pressed state from what stands on it now and keeps no memory of the previous occupant. All moves of the transaction have their positions written before any Switch is read. The switch update runs only in the settle step after the write (and at level load), so a Switch the hero leaves is never seen released before the swapped object stands on it. The settle step first runs the protection pass, then the switch update, then the beam update, which retraces every light from the cells as they are after the write (every settle, not only when a beam carrier moved). So a Rock swapped into a cell on a beam stops or turns that beam in the same update, and a Switch pressed by a swapped object is read before the beam changes; the second switch update of the settle sees what the new beam did. The swap has no handling of riders, and a TELEPORT move gives its riders no SUPPORT move ([Lilies](MOVEMENT.md#lilies)).
- A swap moves only the swapped things (the same holds for a pad and a mirror teleport). What stands on them stays in its cell and falls if nothing holds it up.
- A swap keeps the target's facing. Its TELEPORT move carries the target's own `theta_target`, rounded to 0, 90, 180 or −90, and sets it back when the move finishes. Only TELEPORT moves write θ, so pushes and pulls keep it too. So a swapped Promesst_Light casts in the same direction.
- Blockers end the line with no swap: a solid voxel (except while the hero is in a THROUGH beam), a column outside the level ([Support](MOVEMENT.md#support)), a Tree, a Sign, a closed Gate, a frozen Freezer, a Wardrobe door edge, the z range [`grid.corner.z`, `grid.corner.z` + `squares_z`], the grid edge in a level without wrapping. Water, air, Fences, Treasure_Display and Egg don't stop it. A Mirror met through an open side turns it. A Mirror that has already turned the line ends it with no swap.
- Wrapping: with `grid.wrapping` the line wraps around the level grid; above the upper-layer z (the map rooms) around the fast-travel box. It ends after one lap, back at the hero's own cell: then he is his own target and nothing moves.
- z band: a standing wizard swaps only with a hit at his own z. If nothing is hit there, z + 1 and then z + 2 are traced: a FALLING hit with a live transaction makes his press wait for it; any other hit, or none, gives a normal step. He never swaps with anything above him. It never traces below him, so a Rock in water (at surface − 1) can't be swapped from the surface.
- With no target the press is a normal step. He won't swap from inside a solid cell outside THROUGH; he walks.
- The line steps by the hero's move vector after the beam powers have been applied, not by the pressed direction. In an ENDPOINT beam (the press parallel to the beam where it hits him) that vector is the whole jump to `endpoint_b` (or to `endpoint_a` against the beam), so the line tests only the cell at the endpoint and then cells at multiples of the same distance (to the grid edge). Targets between him and the endpoint, such as a MIRRORABLE Rock lying in the beam, are skipped and stay. With a target at the endpoint he swaps with it; with none he lands on the endpoint. The same holds for DOUBLE beams (the vector is 2ⁿ or 3ⁿ).
- A FALLING wizard swaps with a hit at his z. With a hit only at z + 1 he swaps only when `Settings.allow_wizard_swap_from_below` is set and he has dropped more than 1.1 below `z_when_falling_started`. The flag is set only in `falloff_intro`; it is off on the overworld.
- A worked example, a skipping stone launched first and then swapped with: [Routes that need these mechanics](MOVEMENT.md#routes-that-need-these-mechanics).
- The `wizard_jailbreak` strings belong to a puzzle level's judge, not to an overworld rule.

PULL (Thief), or STRONG + ALTER:

- Applies when the move is player input (CONTROLLER_PROC) and the hero's own step succeeded.
- The first non-DEAD Promesst_Light, Rock, Guy, Mirror, Friendly_Dragon, Rover or Monster in the cell behind him moves into his old cell as PULLED, if that move passes its move check and the thing is not stuck. A Gem there is pulled with the Guy holding it, else the Gem.
- A push and a pull can happen in one move. Sounds: `pull_block`, `pull_mirror`, `pull_gem`, `pull_monster`, `char_%_movedByOther`.
- The pull wraps with the same rule as the line and every other move target ([Wrapping](WORLD.md#wrapping)).

## CHARM

- Each turn an active CHARM Guy (Bard) charms the other Guys, the Monsters, Friendly_Dragons, `mirrorable` Mirrors and MIRRORABLE Rocks within dx, dy, dz ∈ [−2, 2] (wrapped Chebyshev: the cube of ±2 cells around him). Plain Rocks and Rovers are never charmed. Dead entities, ones that don't exist and ones frozen in a Freezer's cell are skipped.
- On a Bard's own move, the charmed Guys, Rocks, Mirrors and Monsters take the same step, each with its own beam powers and move check. The move drops an entity farther than 2.1 from the Bard (wrapped Chebyshev, max(|dx|, |dy|, |dz|); above the upper-layer z it wraps in the fast-travel box), a FALLING one, a SKIPPING Rock and a stuck one (unless THROUGH). Friendly_Dragons are marked as charmed but never move.
- A charmed mover other than a Rock or Mirror turns to face its step (`Single_Move.flags` TURNING, `turning_theta_target` the step's angle), so charmed Guys and Monsters turn.
- A SKIPPING stone is dropped from the move, not moved: its skip is neither cancelled nor turned. SKIPPING is set when the skip starts, together with `time_started_skipping`, and cleared only when a hop ends the skip ("stone_stops_skipping", or the stone sinks or lands). So it holds through every hop ([Skipping stones](MOVEMENT.md#skipping-stones)).
- Order: the charmed list, with the Bard added if he is missing, sorted ascending by key = 1000·⌊dz + 0.5⌋ − trunc(50·(clamp(dot, −2, 2) + 1)). dz is the entity's z minus the Bard's; dot is its wrapped x/y offset from the Bard dotted with the normalised x/y move direction. So lower layers move first, and within a layer the one furthest ahead moves first. The Bard's own key is −50. The sort is a quicksort, so equal keys have no fixed order. Each entry, in sorted order, gets its beam powers and its move check against the moves already added. The Bard's entry is a CONTROLLER_PROC move, the others CHARMED.
- If the Bard can't move, nothing moves. When his own move is missing or under 0.5 long, the transaction is cut back to its size before the charm: only moves of his own (`manipulator_id` the Bard) of type STATIONARY, GATE_OPEN or GATE_CLOSE survive, and he bumps ("go_state_blocked_no_obstacle"). When instead his water check fails, every charmed mover gets a STATIONARY move flagged BARD_MOVING_CANNOT_GO.
- Nothing in a charm move pushes. The Bard's move check keeps a push list, but it and every charmed mover's run with pushing off, so the move check's own SMASH test runs ([Promesst beams and powers](MOVEMENT.md#promesst-beams-and-powers)): a Rock or Rover in the way that is not moving away in the transaction gets a SMASH move if the mover has SMASH and no ALTER (the same fence-edge and frozen-Freezer exceptions), goes on the push list, and the move fails. The bump over the push list runs as well: over the Bard's own list when his move is missing or under 0.5, over the merged lists of all charmed movers when the charm goes ahead. It gives each entry its pusher's SMASH or OPEN effect, so a blocking Rock can get a second SMASH move.
- An entity standing on another that moves in the same charm move follows that move and then makes its own step, so a Bard on a charmed crystal moves 2 cells while the crystal moves 1. His water check is made at the 2-cell target. A pending move of a solid entity or Mirror counts as support there when it ends within 0.5 and between surface − 1.1 and the cell's z + 0.1. In open sea the crystal ends 1 cell behind, the check fails and the whole charm group is cancelled.
- He rides exactly 1 cell when his own step is blocked, and goes 2 cells per step while a row of charmed crystals lies ahead (one more crystal per step). Stepping onto a charmed crystal moves it away unless it is blocked. A crystal can't carry a Bard across open water.

## TRANSMUTE and the other skills

- TRANSMUTE (Druid): a hero who bumps a Tree, Monster, Guy, Friendly_Dragon or a mirrorable Rock, Rover or Mirror turns it into a crystal Rock in place (`transmuted_from_entity_type`, meshes `GAME_SOKO_Crystal_A` / `GAME_SOKO_Crystal_Dragon_A`). Bumping the crystal turns it back ("transmute_stone"), by its `transmuted_from_entity_type`: ROVER, MIRROR, MONSTER, GUY and FRIENDLY_DRAGON rebuild that type; TREE makes a Tree with the stashed mesh and tints; NONE also makes a Tree, with a random one of 5 tree meshes and one of 4 tint pairs. So a level-data crystal, which has no type, turns into a Tree. The new Tree keeps the crystal's position and its MIRRORED flag. Crystals are pushable rocks that fall like rocks: a Tree in water becomes a stepping stone just under the surface; a transmuted dragon breathes no fire. The save records each change as CREATION_OR_DESTRUCTION actions with runtime Pids (namespace 1023). Tree-root fences are created and destroyed with their tree.
- A hero walking into a Friendly_Dragon that follows him swaps places with it (`SWAP_WITH_DRAGON`).
- TAKE_GEMS: Gems don't block him and don't hold him up ([Support](MOVEMENT.md#support)), so he walks or falls into a Gem's cell. There is no automatic pick-up. Taking and dropping is its own input, T ("TakeOrDrop", request TAKE_OR_DROP 5), queued like a press; the same input enters a level from a Level_Entry ([Winning and other inputs](#winning-and-other-inputs)). For each controlled Guy: if his cell holds a free Gem (`inventory_index` < 0), he takes the last free one in the cell list, an INVENTORY_TAKE (15) move ("GemDustoff", "promesst_gem_take"). Otherwise, only if his cell holds a Gem_Slot, he drops the carried Gem with the highest `inventory_index` (the latest taken; ties go to the first in the Gem list), an INVENTORY_DROP (16) move. With neither, nothing happens. The block tests no skill; Gems block other heroes, so in practice only a TAKE_GEMS hero stands on one. A take counts in Status counter 0, a drop in counter 1 ([Status.sokoban_status fields](PROGRESSION.md#statussokoban_status-fields)).
- A taken Gem gets `attached_to_entity_id` = the Guy and `inventory_index` = the highest carried + 1 (or 0), and leaves its grid cell. So no cell lookup sees it: a carried Gem presses no Switch, fills no Gem_Slot, supports and blocks nothing ([Gates, switches and buttons](MOVEMENT.md#gates-switches-and-buttons)).
- QUEEN (on the default hero, 258), SAILOR, TRADER: identity flags. The HUD draws a marker over Guys without QUEEN; SAILOR and TRADER pick switch icons (`UI_CharSwitchSailors` / `UI_CharSwitchTraders`) and voices. QUEEN has no move, switch or win effect. The game tests the bit in two places only: when the prelude completes, `clear_non_prelude_items` Markers clear what is inside them except QUEEN Guys; and the HUD marker.
- PROTECTION: [Goblins, dragons and PROTECTION](MOVEMENT.md#goblins-dragons-and-protection). Push strength: [Pushing and pulling](MOVEMENT.md#pushing-and-pulling). PROTECTION + MULTIPUSH pushes rows while protected; PROTECTION alone, or with PULL or TELEPORT, can't push.
- Artifacts: `Artifact.kind` PROMESST_1/2/3_WAND set `inventory_flags` WAND, TORC, PRISM on pickup. A Prism Artifact's `p3_wand_flags` (colour bits such as OPEN, SMASH, DOUBLE, ALTER) are added to the hero's `inventory_flags` as well: they are the colours his Prism can set (level data; heroes hold no colour bits otherwise); Artifacts stand in puzzle levels, none on the overworld; there 13 inventory-only Wardrobes give the Rod (WAND, 9 of them) or the Torc (TORC, 4). Status counts wand, torc and prism uses (counters 5-7). ACTIVATE runs the item use for every Guy of the active hero: holding any item first adds a STATIONARY move, then Prism, Torc and Rod are used in that order, each on its own, so one ACTIVATE uses every held item. The Rod and the Prism act on the cell ahead: one step along the hero's `theta_target`, snapped to the 4 sides and wrapped like any move.
  - Rod (WAND), the target: dead entities in the cell ahead are ignored. The use fails ("p1wand_fail") if that cell is solid, or holds a Tree, a closed Gate or a frozen Freezer, or holds none of Rock, Gem, Monster, Rover, Promesst_Light, Guy, Artifact and Mirror. A cell off the grid does nothing (only the "WandFailure" event). Otherwise the target is the first of those 8 types in cell-list order. It also fails when the target already has a move in a player-blocking transaction.
  - Rod, the launch: a scan along the facing sends the target to the first cell past it that is not blocked. Blocked means a solid voxel, a Tree, a closed Gate, a frozen Freezer, or the target of a move already in this use. Blocked cells are jumped over, not refused. Fences, Wardrobe doors, water and other types are ignored. If the landing cell holds one of the 8 types, that entity is launched the same way from there: a chain, not a push, with no weight, push strength or move check. If the scan leaves the grid of a level without wrapping, the whole use fails. If it wraps back to the hero's cell, the last thing lands there and the hero moves one cell on. Each moved entity gets a 0.28 s TELEPORT move ("WandSuccess").
  - Prism: acts on the first Promesst_Light in the cell ahead, with no `powered` or dead test. Its `color` moves to the next `Promesst_Power` value, going up and wrapping after 9, whose colour bit (1 << value) the hero holds in `inventory_flags`: OPEN 0, THROUGH 1, SMASH 2, DOUBLE 3, STRONG 4, ENDPOINT 5, ALTER 7, GEM 8, SQUEEZE 9, then OPEN, skipping colours he doesn't hold (6 is XXX_DEPRECATED_EGG_HATCH). With no light ahead: "p3wand_fail". A hero holding no colour bit would loop forever (inferred).
  - Torc: turns the beams hitting the hero to his facing (not traced, [Out of scope](UNCONFIRMED.md#out-of-scope)).

## Winning and other inputs

- Win, checked in the level update after moves settle, not on input: every existing Guy stands, alive and not moving, on an exit Door (`special_door_type` NONE) whose `enabled_state` is ENABLED, and the number of Guys equals the number of exit Doors. A Guy or Door counts only when its PLAYABLE_IN_PRELUDE flag matches the Level_State `misc_flags` DOING_PRELUDE: during a prelude only prelude heroes and prelude Doors, afterwards only the others. Any exit Door that is BROKEN_SOFT or BROKEN_HARD makes the level unwinnable. A DEAD Guy, or a Guy on a Level_Entry instead, blocks the win. A Guy on an exit Door gets `status_flags` ON_EXIT ("on_exit"); leaving clears it ("stop_exiting"). The win runs `handle_level_won`, which sets SOLVED.
- `special_door_type` END1_EXIT wins END1 levels; INTRO_FINAL_EXIT starts the intro ending. In OVERWORLD and INTRO worlds a single hero on a Door with `target_link` is sent on instead.
- The prelude: a level with `Settings.has_prelude` starts with DOING_PRELUDE set ([Existence groups](ENTITIES.md#existence-groups)). Winning it needs no input:
  - `handle_level_won` skips the per-hero solve bookkeeping and the save and next-level step, and starts a 0.5 s countdown. When it runs out, DOING_PRELUDE sends the level to the prelude-end state, which waits 1.0 s and then completes the prelude. The clock is frame time (inferred).
  - With RETURN_TO_PRELUDE set the prelude restarts instead ("actually_restart_prelude"; the prelude Groups exist again). With ESCAPE_FROM_PRELUDE the level ends normally; a hero on a special Door in an END1 level sets it.
  - Completing the prelude ("actually_complete_prelude"), in order: every Group with `is_prelude_group` stops existing (its members are not destroyed); each `clear_non_prelude_items` or `clear_non_prelude_items_inside_freezers` Marker destroys the items inside its box (within 0.5 of it with `is_point_marker`), except QUEEN Guys and items in a cell holding a `dont_clear_non_prelude_item_here` Marker; DOING_PRELUDE is cleared; every non-prelude entity except Particle_Source is re-checked for falling, and one whose supporter is none, a prelude entity or gone has its support cleared first. So whatever the prelude floor held falls ([Falling](MOVEMENT.md#falling)).
  - The items a clear Marker destroys are heroes, Gems, Mirrors and Artifacts, and probably Lilies and Rocks (inferred).
- ACTIVATE: if no GATE_OPEN or GATE_CLOSE move is pending, the magic-mirror activation runs ([Magic mirrors](MOVEMENT.md#magic-mirrors), all or nothing). Then ACTIVATE joins the input queue as a request with no direction ([A move](MOVEMENT.md#a-move)), like a press. When a mirror activation happened, the post-move processing runs first, and the request is queued only if that succeeds. It also fires an Activator in the hero's cell ([Activators and Spawners](MOVEMENT.md#activators-and-spawners)).
- Entering a level: on the overworld a level is entered only by the TakeOrDrop action (T, [TRANSMUTE and the other skills](#transmute-and-the-other-skills)) while exactly one living controlled Guy stands on an enabled Level_Entry. Standing on the cell never enters by itself, so the hero and the things he pushes cross Level_Entry cells freely ([A move](MOVEMENT.md#a-move)). An Artifact pickup path into a level exists only in puzzle levels.
- Hero switch: `status_flags` ACTIVE moves to another hero; nothing else moves; skipping stones keep going.
  - Roster: rebuilt at level load, undo and prelude events, one entry per group: the Guys whose `turn_order_id` is their own `entity_id` (unset defaults to it). Guys sharing a `turn_order_id` move as one hero. Left out: `scheduled_for_destruction`; IN_EXISTENCE_GROUP without CURRENTLY_EXISTS; EXISTS_FOR_COSMETIC_TRANSITION_PURPOSES_ONLY; a Guy whose PLAYABLE_IN_PRELUDE doesn't match the Level_State `misc_flags` DOING_PRELUDE.
  - Order: by `Guy.turn_order`, ascending. An unset value (−1) becomes 100 × (`squares_y` − 1 − ⌊y + 0.5⌋) + ⌊x + 0.5⌋, from the raw position (`grid.corner` is not subtracted). So row 0 is the high-y edge, y = `squares_y` − 1, and columns run in +x: reading order with north at the top.
  - The switch key ("key_char_toggle") takes the next entry, wrapping, that still has a living, existing Guy. All-DEAD and mid-transmute entries are skipped; ON_EXIT and FALLING Guys are not. With none, the hero stays.
  - Character hotkeys cycle the entries of one character and its _ALT (WARRIOR 11, THIEF 17, WIZARD 12, PRIESTESS 13, SAILOR 14, TRADER 15, PROMESST 16).
  - Auto switch: at turn end the game lists the active entry's Guys, DEAD ones included. It leaves out only Guys that don't exist, are on the wrong side of the prelude, or are TRANSMUTING with no progress yet. If the list is empty, it takes the next entry with a living Guy. So a dead hero does not trigger it; it fires only when the active entry's Guys are gone.
- Undo steps back one record; reset puts every entity back in one record.

## Move flags

- `Move_Info.flags`: DISAPPEAR_AFTER_TELEPORT 1, TELEPORT_EMITTERS_ALREADY_HANDLED 2, STRONG_PUSH 4, MOVED_BY_MIRROR 8, UPDATE_SUPPORT_DESPITE_STATIONARY 0x10, SUPPORT_WILL_BE_LOST 0x20, TELEPORTING_FORWARD 0x40, DISAPPEAR_DUE_TO_LEAVING_THE_MAP 0x100, APPEAR_AFTER_TELEPORT 0x200, SKIPPING_ODD_PARITY_REBOUND 0x400, MERGE_WITH_MANIPULATOR 0x800, TRANSMUTE_CREATE 0x1000, TRANSMUTE_DESTROY 0x2000, DIAGONAL_LILY_PUSH 0x4000, BARD_MOVING_CANNOT_GO 0x8000, TELEPORT_FALL_STATIONARY_MOVE 0x10000, TELEPORT_FALL_WILL_BLOCK_PLAYER 0x20000. `Single_Move.flags` is a separate `Flags` enum.
- `Move_Transaction` flags: CLOSED 1, PHYSICALLY_MOVED 2, BLOCKING_PLAYER 4, PRE_CLOSED 8, DONE 0x10, DRIVEN_BY_A_CONTROLLER 0x20, IS_LILY_TRANSACTION 0x40, CAUSED_CHANGE 0x80, IS_FALLING_TRANSACTION 0x100, IS_ROVER_TRANSACTION 0x200, DO_NOT_RECORD_UNDO 0x400, PLAYER_TELEPORTING_OUT_OF_FALL 0x800 (the hero acts mid-fall), IS_MERGE_RESULT 0x1000, DRAGON_BREATH_BLOCKED 0x2000, BLOCKING_PLAYER_ONLY_UNTIL_PHYSICAL_MOVE 0x10000, IS_SKIPPING_TRANSACTION 0x20000.
- `Move_Type`: TELEPORT 1, STATIONARY 2, FALLING 3, ELEVATOR 4, CHARMED 5, PULLED 6, PUSHED 7, LINEAR_INTERPOLATION 8, SUPPORT 9, FOLLOWING 10, CONTROLLER_PROC 11, TRANSMUTE 14, INVENTORY_TAKE 15, INVENTORY_DROP 16, GATE_OPEN 17, GATE_CLOSE 18, LILY 19, SKIP 20, SMASH 21.
