# Entities

The overworld's entity types, the existence groups that make entities appear and vanish together, and the collectibles.

The cell lookup that every gameplay test uses (moves, support, pushes, contacts) skips some entities; [Existence groups](#existence-groups).

## Existence groups

- A `Group` with `is_existence_group` makes its members appear and vanish together.
- Load: every existence Group, prelude Groups and the rest alike, gets `change_existence_group_status(exists_by_default, force)`. `exists` becomes `exists_by_default`; the stored `exists` is ignored. Every direct member (`base.group_id` = the Group, EXISTS_FOR_COSMETIC_TRANSITION_PURPOSES_ONLY members left out) gets IN_EXISTENCE_GROUP and CURRENTLY_EXISTS = the status; switching a Group off clears CURRENTLY_EXISTS on every member but Inanimate ones. It recurses into member existence Groups. The CURRENTLY_EXISTS stored on members in the level data is overwritten. The same pass sets `Level_State.misc_flags` DOING_PRELUDE when `Settings.has_prelude` is set.
- So a member exists at load iff its Group exists by default. Some entities outside existence Groups carry IN_EXISTENCE_GROUP + CURRENTLY_EXISTS and keep them.
- Prelude rooms stand on floor Fences (`blocking`, `is_floor`) that are PLAYABLE_IN_PRELUDE and members of the prelude Group. PLAYER_BLOCKING_ONLY Ocean_Maps may cover holes in that floor ([Water](movement.md#water)). Completing the prelude switches the prelude Groups off and re-checks what they held ([Winning and other inputs](skills.md#winning-and-other-inputs)).
- When the history loads, every existence Group whose restored `exists` differs from the status applied at load gets `change_existence_group_status(exists, force)` again, which reaches its members and subgroups. A save's Wall_Set existence CHANGEs in the map rooms go 0 → 1.
- Making a Group exist recurses into its existence subgroups, except a subgroup named by `unexist_this_id`/`2`/`3` of a Group that exists at that moment. Nothing else unexists a Group. The `X_*` connector caps in the map rooms work this way: a child of one area, unexisted by the neighbouring area.
- What toggles Groups: Teleporter enable (`group_id`, `also_exist_this_group_id`), `FastTravelEnableGroup` Markers, a Switch with `existence_group` (none on the overworld), story and subtitle events (`cage_disappear`, `magistrate_group`, `egg_hatch`), Judge puzzles, RubbleDisappear, Hedron/Activate, level load and transitions.
- Entering existence sets CURRENTLY_EXISTS and clears INVISIBLE_IN_A_TOGGLABLE_WAY (the reverse on leaving); Ocean_Map members are rebuilt then. Every existence-group Ocean_Map is in the map rooms.
- On playable layers only a few Fences, Teleporters, Groups and a View_Giver are group members. So a save's existence flips are almost all in the map rooms (z ≥ 75) and on map-room twins.
- Entity-exists test: with IN_EXISTENCE_GROUP, an entity exists iff CURRENTLY_EXISTS; without it, unless EXISTS_FOR_COSMETIC_TRANSITION_PURPOSES_ONLY.
- A `Group` whose `demo_existence` names a demo phase (`EXISTS_IF_DEMO1`, `EXISTS_IF_DEMO2`, `EXISTS_IF_DEMO2_OR_3`, `EXISTS_IF_DEMO3`) and everything in it are absent from the full game. The build scripts drop them when reading the package.
- The shared cell lookup (support, the landing and water move check, hero contacts, ...) skips: an entity IN_EXISTENCE_GROUP without CURRENTLY_EXISTS; one with EXISTS_FOR_COSMETIC_TRANSITION_PURPOSES_ONLY; one `scheduled_for_destruction`; a NOT_SNAPPED_TO_GRID one other than a Door, wherever it stands; a PLAYABLE_IN_PRELUDE one unless `Level_State.misc_flags` has DOING_PRELUDE. Fence blocking goes through it too. Skipped by the lookup is not absent: the beam update's light loop has the exists and prelude test but no snap test, so a NOT_SNAPPED_TO_GRID light still casts its beam.
- Code that walks a type's entity list directly applies the same exists and prelude test: weights, the hero roster, the move commit, Gates and Switches, light power and beams, the water list, Lilies, dragon attacks, Rovers and Doors all skip absent entities.
- These don't skip, so an absent entity still takes part:
    - the discovered-teleporter carry-over ([Teleporters in the save](state.md#teleporters-in-the-save)): an absent pad that was discovered and enabled is applied;
    - the TAB toggle's search for a FAST_TRAVEL box ([Wrapping](world.md#wrapping));
    - an Area_Pin's activation: once 2 or more pins of the hero's layer class are activated, every untaken View_Giver of that class is taken, an absent one too;
    - the walls rebuild's floor Fences and Ocean_Maps: absent ones still put their column inside the level ([Support](movement.md#support));
    - the GEM beam's slot gather ([Promesst beams and powers](movement.md#promesst-beams-and-powers)): an absent carrier powers a slot and an absent slot gets powered;
    - Lily movement groups: an absent Lily next to a moving Lily joins its group, and one at a drifting Lily's target stops the drift;
    - the Freezer reopen and unlock passes: absent map-room Freezers open with the rest.
- Entities absent at load are almost all in the map rooms (z ≥ 75). On a playable layer only a floor Fence is absent.

| Map scrolls       | `View_Giver` (one per layer): taking one adds its `map_overview_id` / `_2` Camera_Controls to `enabled_alternate_views`; a layer's TAB overhead map needs that layer's camera. Saved as `activation_order` ≠ 0 plus INVISIBLE_IN_A_TOGGLABLE_WAY. No fog effect |
