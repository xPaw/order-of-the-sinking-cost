# Movement

How a move resolves (support, falling, water, pushing and pulling) and every mechanic that moves things: gates, rocks,
skipping stones, monsters, beams, mirrors, rovers, lilies, rivers, elevators, pads and Spawners.

## A move

- A key press queues a move: a per-level input queue of at most 6 requests, extra presses dropped, run one at a time. ACTIVATE goes into the same queue, with no direction. A move starts only when no in-flight transaction is BLOCKING_PLAYER. Every hero move is BLOCKING_PLAYER until DONE; Lily, Rover and falling transactions don't block, elevator moves do. A move into the hero's target cell by a Rock, Rover, Lily, Guy or a few other types blocks the press until it lands.
- The move check tests the destination cell for solid voxels, blocking entities and fences on the edge (start cell side and destination side), not the cells in between (DOUBLE and ENDPOINT moves skip cells). The fence-edge test is skipped for a TELEPORT move and for a mover with SQUEEZE, so a pad or a magic-mirror teleport lands across a Fence; the Wardrobe door test is not skipped.
- Occupants that never block a move: Door, Switch, Rubble, Flat_Decoration, Lily, Elevator_Platform, Gem_Slot, Teleporter, Level_Entry, an unfrozen Freezer and a DEAD Monster. So a pushed Rock enters a Level_Entry's cell as it enters a Switch's.
- The move check runs for the TELEPORT and ENDPOINT-jump check (no pushes), the normal step (with pushes), PULL, a hero on a drifting Lily stepping toward an oncoming Lily ([Lilies](#lilies)), and CHARM ([CHARM](skills.md#charm)).
- A hero inside a solid voxel, a Tree's cell or a frozen Freezer's cell, outside a THROUGH beam, is stuck and cannot move.
- No climbing: the hero never steps up. There is no fall damage.
- A transaction writes its positions at its physical time and is DONE when its duration has elapsed. All pushes of a row are in the hero's transaction and are written together at his physical time.

## Support

An entity with DOES_NOT_FALL in `entity_flags` is always supported: the support check returns at once, and the support
update, the fall test and the turn's fall list all skip it. Any other entity is supported when the support check finds
any of these (the `protected` fallback runs before the water and implicit-floor tests):

- its own cell is solid (stuck in a wall; see the solid query below), over water and mid-air alike;
- a solid voxel below;
- in the probe cell (x, y, z − d + 1), where d is the drop (1, or the DOUBLE multiplier in a powered fall): a Lily (for every non-Lily entity), an Elevator_Platform or a Fence with `blocking` and `is_floor`, for any d; a Tree only when d < 1.1;
- below it: a Rock, Sign, Tree, Gem, Rover, Judge, Promesst_Light, Treasure_Display, closed non-FLAT Gate, frozen Freezer, Monster, Guy, Friendly_Dragon or `is_vertical` Mirror. A Promesst_Light block supports; its beam never does;
- for a Guy with `protected`: anywhere, over air too;
- the implicit floor. Dry square: supported at z ≤ the layer base, in the ground layer and the map rooms only (with an Annihilator, only on OVERWORLD and not underground). So the overworld has a dry floor at z ≤ 0 on the ground and in the map rooms, none underground or in the sky. Water square: supported only below the ground floor: at z < `ground_z0` outside the underworld band (`underground_z0` up to `underground_barrier`), and at z < `underground_z0`; on the overworld z −4..−1 and z < −25. Any Ocean_Map square, PLAYER_BLOCKING_ONLY included, is a water square here, so nothing stands on a PLAYER_BLOCKING_ONLY map's surface: a Rock pushed over it falls through, and the hero's step onto it must pass the water step check ([Water](#water)).

The check ignores the entity itself, DEAD entities, anything `scheduled_for_destruction`, FALLING entities (unless the caller allows them) and a Gem under a TAKE_GEMS Guy, who falls into its cell ([Pushing and pulling](#pushing-and-pulling)). Every entity type is held the same way. A kill does not recheck what the body held up: support and falls are checked when an entity's own move finishes, and for everything at once by the level-wide sweep (a support check of every entity, then a fall for each unsupported one, lowest first). The sweep runs on a fresh level load (the overworld's first load), when a level is restored to its start state and at the story's first ending. It does not run after an ordinary undo or when the hero returns to the overworld from a puzzle level; the return destroys every Level_Entry whose `currently_broken` or `currently_disfavored` is set and re-registers Lilies with a `moving_direction`. So a Mirror on a goblin that dies stays put until it moves or the overworld's next fresh load.

The check does not see a vertical ENDPOINT leg: a Guy in one is unsupported, and the fall fails ([Promesst beams and powers](#promesst-beams-and-powers)). In a DOUBLE beam the check looks d cells down.

The solid query reads the voxels and the level's column mask. A column is outside the level when it has no WALLS Wall_Set voxel, no Ocean_Map square and no floor Fence (`is_floor`); a voxel above or below the grid's z range still puts its column inside ([The grid](gamedata.md#the-grid)). With an Annihilator every column counts as inside. A cell in the grid whose column is outside the level is solid at every z; a cell outside the grid is solid only below `grid.corner.z`.

## Falling

- An unsupported entity falls one cell per step until it meets support, with no height limit and no damage to the faller. A DOUBLE beam scales the step; an ENDPOINT beam parallel to the fall turns it into a jump to `endpoint_a`, which a blocked landing cancels ([Promesst beams and powers](#promesst-beams-and-powers)).
- In a water square the fall stops at surface − 1, on every layer and at any depth: a step is cancelled when the next z satisfies `surface − 3 < tz < surface − 1`. The water is a non-PLAYER_BLOCKING_ONLY map in tz's layer with map z ≤ tz + 2.1.
- A Lily floats at the surface (supported while z ≤ surface + 0.5), never on a PLAYER_BLOCKING_ONLY map. No other entity floats. A protected Guy never falls.
- Settle step: runs after the positions of every move transaction are written (the player's move, then each fall, kill and Gate move). An ACTIVATE input is itself the move, so a magic-mirror teleport is written before the protection and attack passes ([Magic mirrors](#magic-mirrors)). In order: the protection pass; the switch and Gate updates; the beam update; the support check of everything that moved, which builds the fall list; the attack pass (goblin attacks and fire: `protected` set for every hit, then the kills); the switch update again if anything changed, then the monster Gate update; a second protection pass and a second attack pass that kills nothing and only turns hits on `pre_protected` Guys into `protected`; the support check of every Guy whose `protected` just cleared; then the falls start, lowest first, skipping entities already in a move and DEAD ones. So a fall reads `protected` as the attack passes leave it: a hero who walks into water and is hit that turn stays at the surface, and a Mirror pushed into water still turns fire that turn before it sinks ([Goblins, dragons and PROTECTION](#goblins-dragons-and-protection)).
- Below the Annihilator's z (−100) a fall step becomes an annihilation.
- A falling Guy or Monster lands on a Guy or Monster below it. A falling solid entity (Rock, Gem, Promesst_Light, Rover, Tree, ...) kills the Guy or Monster in the target cell, unless the fall is short (`z_when_falling_started` under 2.1 above the target) and that one moves away in the same move. A falling entity smashes a flat Mirror it lands on.
- On the overworld's ground and sky a Guy may step off any dry ledge into air and falls to the first support. The move check refuses a dry ledge step only in two cases: in the UNDERGROUND layer of an OVERWORLD level it needs support below, down to −26.1, or a matching jump marker (else the hero would fall to the Annihilator); and with `Settings.prevent_walking_into_annihilator` (a few non-overworld levels) it needs support down to z −0.1.
- A lily drop is a FALLING move; riders follow as SUPPORT moves. Leaving the grid sets DISAPPEAR_DUE_TO_LEAVING_THE_MAP.

## Water

- Water query: the existing Ocean_Maps sorted by z, highest first. A lookup takes the first map with bit 0 of `ocean_values` at the square, in the position's ground layer, with map z ≤ pos.z + 2.1. `PLAYER_BLOCKING_ONLY` maps count only when the caller asks: lily floating, drowning and the fall's water landing skip them; the support check's water test and the hero's step check include them. Such a map holds no one up and lets nothing float, and the hero steps onto it only where the step check below passes. The overworld's are all in the map rooms; in prelude levels they can cover holes in a prelude room's floor ([Existence groups](entities.md#existence-groups)).
- `ocean_values` per square: bit 0 water, bit 1 `River_Polarity` (0 TURNS_RIGHT, 1 TURNS_LEFT), bits 2-4 flow `Direction` (1 E, 2 N, 3 W, 4 S). Square index `(x − corner.x) + (y − corner.y) × squares_x` in the map's local grid, turned by the map's orientation ([Ocean_Map (water)](gamedata.md#ocean_map-water)), x/y rounded with +0.5 floor, wrapped. Voxels of an `OCEAN_HOLE` Wall_Set whose `ocean_hole_id` is the map's pid clear bit 0.
- Drowning: after each move settles, a Guy or Monster whose water surface (found from z + 2, non-PLAYER_BLOCKING_ONLY map) is in [z + 0.5, z + 2.5], 1 or 2 cells under the surface, dies, unless its `supported_by_id` is an Elevator_Platform. Deeper water is invisible to the move, fall and drown checks. PROTECTION doesn't prevent drowning.
- So a Guy that falls into water ends at surface − 1 and drowns, a Monster pushed in drowns (Monsters skip the water move check), and a Rock pushed in becomes a bridge with its top at the surface, on every layer.
- Stepping into water (Guys and Rovers; Monsters skip it): for a Guy that isn't protected the step check walks the cells from the target z down to surface − 0.1 and runs the full support check ([Support](#support)) on the hero at each; the first cell that passes accepts the step. A floor Fence in the target cell at or above surface − 0.1 accepts it, and so does a target inside a solid voxel (a THROUGH step). The step is allowed when any of these holds:
    - support at some z′ in that range;
    - for such a z′, in the same loop, the cell is attacked (a goblin within 1.1, or on a dragon's traced segment) and the hero has PROTECTION, `pre_protected` or `protected`, or a PROTECTION Guy is within 1.1;
    - a Mirror or Gem in the cell below z′ (map not PLAYER_BLOCKING_ONLY), tested in the same loop;
    - a Promesst_Light in the cell under the target;
    - another entity's pending move (a solid entity or Mirror) ending within 0.5 horizontally of the target with z in [surface − 1.1, target z + 0.1);
    - the hero rides a supporter moved by another transaction;
    - the hero stands at the surface z and a powered DOUBLE beam's position sample is within 0.1 of the target.
- A protected Guy may always step into water, except in the UNDERGROUND layer of an OVERWORLD level; with `Settings.prevent_walking_into_annihilator` the step must pass that check too ([Falling](#falling)). The step check reads `protected` as the previous turn left it ([Goblins, dragons and PROTECTION](#goblins-dragons-and-protection)).
- A `may_jump_into_water` Marker (overworld sky and underworld edges) allows the step when the move direction · its listed direction ≥ 0.5, the hero is more than 0.5 above the surface and the map is not INVISIBLE. The hero then falls to surface − 1 and drowns: every overworld jump marker is a death edge, never a route.
- A river is never drained, only redirected ([Rivers](#rivers)). Only `ocean_resetter` Spawners can take water away again ([Activators and Spawners](#activators-and-spawners)).

## Pushing and pulling

- Walking into a Rock, Guy, Monster, Mirror, Gem, Rover, Promesst_Light or Friendly_Dragon pushes it one cell, and it pushes whatever is in its own way. Trees, Signs, Judges, Treasure_Displays and walls are never pushed: they block. The move check skips DEAD occupants, and it builds the push in the same loop, so a dead body neither blocks nor is pushed.
- Push strength s = STRONG beam count + (MULTIPUSH ? 2 : SINGLE_PUSH ? 1 : 0). s ≥ 1 can push, s ≥ 2 can multipush. STRONG + ALTER cancels pushing and gives PULL. STRONG + SINGLE_PUSH works like MULTIPUSH.
- Weight W: each pushed thing counts 1, plus 1 for everything stacked on it (found through what supports what). Not counted: Switch, Lily, Artifact, Door, Gate, Rubble, dead Guys and Monsters, Flat_Decoration, Level_Entry, Elevator_Platform, Gem_Slot, Teleporter, an unfrozen Freezer, FLAT entities, anything `scheduled_for_destruction`. A following Friendly_Dragon (`following_id` ≠ 0) ends the count. W ≥ 1 needs s ≥ 1, W ≥ 2 needs s ≥ 2; otherwise the move is a bump ("no_multipush"). The same count decides the weight crush and `elevator_too_heavy` ([Elevators](#elevators)).
- So SINGLE_PUSH moves one object with nothing countable on top; MULTIPUSH moves a row and stacks of any length. A Wardrobe skill replaces SINGLE_PUSH and keeps MULTIPUSH, so a PULL, TELEPORT or PROTECTION hero without MULTIPUSH can't push ([Wardrobes](skills.md#wardrobes)).
- Distance: one cell. A pusher's step longer than 1.1 (DOUBLE, a jump) is cut to one cell per axis for the push. The push then goes through the power solver with the pushed entity's own beam colours (Guys, Monsters, MIRRORABLE Rocks, `mirrorable` Mirrors and Rovers), so its own DOUBLE beams scale it.
- Weight crush: tested once per turn after the end-of-turn updates, on Guys without MULTIPUSH, Rovers and Monsters only; Rocks and Mirrors are never crushed. Entities that don't exist ([Existence groups](entities.md#existence-groups)), and PLAYABLE_IN_PRELUDE ones outside the prelude, are skipped.
    - Crush strength s: a Guy has STRONG + 1 with SINGLE_PUSH, else STRONG. A Monster and a `mirrorable` Rover have STRONG + 1; a plain Rover has 1. STRONG and ALTER together give 0. MULTIPUSH plays no part. One STRONG beam makes a Monster or a `mirrorable` Rover immune (s ≥ 2).
    - W is the entity itself plus everything it supports, counted as for the push weight. It is crushed when W − 1 > s. So a SINGLE_PUSH Guy dies under 2 or more things, and a Guy with no push skill under 1.
    - A crushed Rover is destroyed and Rubble (`rubble_type` ROVER) takes its place. A crushed Guy or Monster dies at once.
    - What rested on it is rechecked for support after the crush tests of Guys, Rovers and Monsters: it is held by something else or falls as in [Falling](#falling), with its own riders.
- A push fails, and the hero bumps, if any pushed thing can't make its own move: a solid voxel at its target (unless that thing is in a THROUGH beam), a fence edge, a Tree, Sign, Judge, Treasure_Display, frozen Freezer or closed Gate in the next cell, a pushable that can't move, the row looping back on itself or to the pusher (wrap), or the pushed thing stuck in a wall. One failure blocks the whole push.
- A refused step (blocked or refused by weight) bumps its push list: each entry that doesn't move gets its pusher's SMASH or OPEN effect ([Promesst beams and powers](#promesst-beams-and-powers)). Entries that move are never bumped.
- A TAKE_GEMS Guy is not blocked by Gems: he walks or falls into the cell. A Friendly_Dragon that follows the mover swaps places with him instead.
- PULL: [TELEPORT and PULL](skills.md#teleport-and-pull). A pushed object falls when unsupported ([Falling](#falling), [Objects in water](#objects-in-water)).

## Gates, switches and buttons

- A Gate blocks its cell while closed, and a closed Gate that is not FLAT holds up what stands in the cell above ([Support](#support)). Under water at surface − 1 it is a bridge: the hero stands on it at the surface and does not drown, and the step into water passes, since the support lies between the target z and surface − 0.1. An open Gate holds up nothing, so a skipping stone passes over it.
- A switch gate is open when every existing Switch of its `flavor` is pressed, xor `notted`. A gate with no existing Switch of its `flavor` (every glyph gate, and a few others) is left alone by the switch update: whatever opens or shuts it stays.
- A Switch is pressed by a Tree, Rock, Monster, Promesst_Light, Mirror, Artifact, Gem or Rover in its cell. A Guy presses only `special_type` NORMAL (every overworld Switch). A closed Gate in its cell holds a NORMAL Switch down. Only what is on the grid presses: a Gem carried by a TAKE_GEMS Guy is off the grid ([TRANSMUTE and the other skills](skills.md#transmute-and-the-other-skills)).
- A Switch is pressed only while something stands in it. The overworld has one Guy ([Entity catalogue](entities.md#entity-catalogue)), so the hero can hold one Switch, not while he walks through its Gate. A switch gate the hero must pass, or a flavor with more than one Switch, needs an object left on each Switch.
- Gate moves (GATE_OPEN, GATE_CLOSE) come from four sources: the OPEN bump, the ALTER bump, the switch update and the monster gate update. So a Gate that is no monster gate and whose flavor has no Switch opens only by an OPEN bump and shuts only by an ALTER bump. A Gate move blocks and frees river squares like a blocker ([Rivers](#rivers)).
- A monster gate (`is_monster_gate`) is open when no living Monster stands in its screen room, xor `notted`. A room is the screen of the grid that holds `floor(x + 0.5)`, `floor(y + 0.5)` (clamped), plus the layer band holding z, else the nearest band below. A Monster counts in any state when it exists (a PLAYABLE_IN_PRELUDE one only during the prelude); goblins and dragons count. The update runs once per turn after the attack pass ([Falling](#falling)): the gate opens when the room's last Monster dies (drowned, crushed, burnt or hit by a goblin) and shuts when a living one is in the room again. A pending GATE_OPEN or GATE_CLOSE counts as the gate's state now.- A closing gate pushes whatever stands in its cell one cell along its closing axis (straight down for an upright gate; a strong push, no weight limit). If that can't move, a Guy or Monster there is killed, and a Rock, Judge, Promesst_Light, Gem, Rover, Tree, Sign, Treasure_Display, Mirror or frozen Freezer is smashed to Rubble; another Gate is ignored. A gate shutting on a hero who stands on a floor kills him.
- OPEN: a mover in an OPEN beam and in no ALTER beam that moves into a closed Gate opens it: the mover bumps (it stays, the turn is used) and the gate gets a GATE_OPEN move. The mover can be a Guy, a MIRRORABLE Rock, a `mirrorable` Mirror or Rover, or a Monster, by a step, push, fall or skip. With ALTER as well, moving into an open Gate shuts it. Either way the gate's `notted` flips, so a switch gate keeps its new state until its switches change. The beam itself opens nothing: it stops at a closed Gate.

## Objects in water

- Whatever is pushed off support into water falls and rests at surface − 1, at any depth and on every layer: a Rock that is not SKIPPABLE, a Gem, a Mirror (plain, magic or vertical), a Promesst_Light, a Rover or a Monster (a Monster drowns there). Only a Friendly_Dragon never starts a fall, so it never sinks.
- What then holds up the hero, or an object pushed onto the cell: a Rock, a Gem, a Promesst_Light, a Rover and a vertical Mirror (`is_vertical`). So the sunk object's top is a floor at the surface: a bridge. A second object pushed over the first sinks in the next water cell, so a gap of several water cells closes with one object per cell.
- A flat Mirror (plain or magic) holds up nothing: whatever lands in its cell from above, the hero included, smashes it (Rubble) and ends at surface − 1. A dead Monster holds nothing either. A TAKE_GEMS hero falls through a Gem ([Pushing and pulling](#pushing-and-pulling)); a sunk Gem holds other objects, and a sunk Mirror does not hold the hero (inferred).
- A skipping stone that sank in place against a wall ([Skipping stones](#skipping-stones)) holds the hero like a sunk Rock, and a magic-mirror hop ([Magic mirrors](#magic-mirrors)) lands on it ([Routes that need these mechanics](#routes-that-need-these-mechanics)).
- A Rock at surface − 1 in a river square blocks the river ([Rivers](#rivers)), as do the other things that settle there; a Rock riding a Lily stands at the surface and does not.
- The sunk object is the only floor there: the water square keeps its value. An Activator reset of the object's Spawner destroys it, which takes the bridge back ([Activators and Spawners](#activators-and-spawners)).

## Skipping stones

- A SKIPPABLE Rock starts skipping after it is moved (pushed or charmed) when nothing supports it, it has a direction (`move_theta` ≠ −1), it is not skipping already, and it stands in a water square whose surface is in [rock z − 0.5, rock z + 0.5). A move flagged START_SKIPPING also starts a skip. An unsupported Rock that fails any condition falls instead.
- Skipping runs in real time, not turns: each skip is a transaction (IS_SKIPPING_TRANSACTION) lasting `Settings.time_per_skip` (the overworld has 0.24 s), and the next one starts when none is in flight, while the hero keeps acting.
- Each skip, in order: supported where it is (no water at the cell, solid below, or a supporting entity below) → it stops; a Lily or Tree in its cell → it stops (on a Lily it rides, and kicks it); a Mirror one cell below → it sinks onto the Mirror, which is smashed to Rubble; else one cell along `move_theta` (beam powers apply) under the normal move check, where other skipping stones don't block. It wraps around the world.
- If the next cell is blocked (a wall at its z, an entity standing there), it sinks in place: unless something already supports it, it takes a one-cell fall step through the power solver and the move check. If that passes, it stops skipping and falls as in [Falling](#falling), ending at surface − 1 whatever the depth, so its top is a floor, also over deep columns.
- Kicks: the stone kicks the Lilies beside it ([Lilies](#lilies)) once per hop that moves it, and at the hop where it sinks in place when that fall passes the move check. It kicks nothing when it is supported where it is, when a Lily or Tree is in its cell (the Lily is kicked along the stone's heading instead), when it sinks from a collision with another skipping stone, or when the sink's fall step fails.
- A skip off the grid still moves; on the 10th the stone leaves the map.
- When two skipping stones meet in a cell, the one with the later `time_started_skipping` (tie: Pid order) sinks immediately.
- A stone that comes to rest on a Lily kicks it ([Lilies](#lilies)). Stones never carry the hero.

## Goblins, dragons and PROTECTION

- A GOBLIN attacks the Guys and Monsters within distance 1.1: its own cell and the six neighbours, above and below included. Other Goblins and dragons are Monsters, so they are hit too; Rovers are not killed. Only a blocking Fence on the crossed edge (a floor Fence, for the cells above and below) blocks the attack, unless the Goblin stands in a SQUEEZE beam: a Fence in the Goblin's cell stops it leaving through that edge and one in the target's cell stops it entering. Walls, objects, closed Gates and other Monsters block nothing. A target stuck inside a wall is skipped, unless the Goblin stands in a THROUGH beam. A Guy with `pre_protected` becomes `protected` instead of dying; every other Guy and Monster hit dies.
- Only a Monster with the DRAGON constant flag breathes fire; a Friendly_Dragon has none. It breathes along its local +x axis rounded to a grid axis (`theta_target` 0 E, 90 N, 180 W, −90 S) and never moves. A dead or transmuted dragon, or one stuck in a wall, breathes none.
- The fire trace (shared with the wizard line) steps one cell at a time at the dragon's z, wraps, has no length limit and runs over water and air. It stops at a solid voxel, a column outside the level ([Support](#support)), a frozen Freezer, a closed Gate, a Tree, a Sign, a Wardrobe door edge, its own dragon or start cell, or outside the grid z range [−102, 110]. It ends in the cell of a Gem, Guy, Rock, Promesst_Light, Monster, Friendly_Dragon or Rover. A Mirror turns it; fire may pass any number of Mirrors but ends at the same Mirror twice. Fences don't stop it. DEAD entities and ones already marked to die are ignored.
- The ray kills the Guys and Monsters in the cell it ends in; a Guy with `pre_protected` gets `protected` (and its turn group) instead. Rocks, lights, Rovers and Friendly_Dragons end the ray and do not die. If a Gem is among what the ray hits in that cell, nothing there is killed.
- Waking: each turn the Monster pass takes the nearest Guy within 3.5 (after a path and water check), else the nearest Rover within 3.5 at the same z. A target makes the Monster ACTIVE, none makes it inactive. The 3.5 gather keeps absent Rovers and absent group Guys ([Existence groups](entities.md#existence-groups)); attacks use the 1.1 list, which skips them.
- A goblin turns toward a target it sees at its own z and stays in its cell. A Mirror carried by a goblin does not turn with it.
- Attacks skip targets outside the goblin's existence group; Mirrors and other entities are never attacked. A goblin attacks whether it is ACTIVE or not (Waking sets only its animation state), and a goblin stuck inside a wall attacks nothing unless it stands in a THROUGH beam. So a Monster pushed beside a Goblin dies in that turn's attack, a Goblin or dragon included, and its death can empty a monster Gate's room ([Gates, switches and buttons](#gates-switches-and-buttons)).
- `pre_protected` = the Guy has PROTECTION (16) in `skill_flags`, or stands within 1.1 of such a Guy (above and below included), or is in the turn group of a Guy that does. A turn group is every existing Guy with the same `turn_order_id`; a Guy with none counts under his own entity id, so the Guys that move as one hero are one group, wherever they stand.
- `protected` is set only when a goblin or gargoyle attack, or fire, hits a `pre_protected` Guy, before anything in that pass dies. It goes onto the victim's whole turn group and onto the turn group of every PROTECTION Guy within 1.1 of the victim; a Guy beside the victim in another turn group gets nothing. It clears each protected Guy's `supported_by`.
- The protection pass runs after the move is enacted ([Falling](#falling)). Its first run in a turn copies `protected` to `was_protected`; every run clears `pre_protected` and `protected` on each Guy that has `pre_protected` when the pass starts, then recomputes `pre_protected`. A Guy who is `protected` without `pre_protected` at that moment (a member of a hit Guy's turn group away from every PROTECTION Guy) keeps `protected` until a later pass finds him `pre_protected`. A move reads `protected` as the previous turn left it. Runtime fields, not saved.
- A protected Guy is held where it stands, over water or air; in water that is the surface. Once `protected` clears it falls to surface − 1 and drowns, so stepping out of fire into water is no route.
- A dry step into an attacked cell is never refused. The attack resolves after the move, from the new positions, against the recomputed `pre_protected`: a hero who steps beside a PROTECTION Guy is safe, and a goblin that moved this turn attacks from its new cell. The only attack test in the move check is the water step's ([Water](#water)).
- The attack pass runs twice in each settle step ([Falling](#falling)); only the first kills. A kill is a move of its own with its own settle step, so by the code one dragon kills every Monster along its line in one input, one per settle step (not checked). All the rays of a pass are traced before any death, so two dragons whose fire lines meet kill each other.
- Rocks, Mirrors and other objects are never attack targets. A hero who steps into a goblin's neighbour cell as its Rock leaves is hit.

## Promesst beams and powers

- A `Promesst_Light` casts its beam while `powered`. Its room is powered when the room has at least one counted Gem_Slot and every counted slot is filled. A slot counts only while it exists. It is filled by a free Gem in its cell (`attached_to_entity_id` 0), or when its `flags` hold BEAM_POWERED (a GEM beam, below). Slot power, and each light's `powered`, is recomputed from the room at load, during turn processing and when a Group's existence changes (if the changed members include a Gem_Slot or a Gem), whatever was stored: a Group that makes an unfilled slot exist turns its room's lights off, and a light in a room with a filled slot is on even where the level data stores `powered` false. A light in a frozen Freezer's cell casts no beam.
- A room is a 19 × 13 screen in the ground, underworld and sky layers, and a whole map-room layer above them ([Screens and rooms](world.md#screens-and-rooms)). So one filled Gem_Slot in a map room powers every light in that map room.
- Magic Mirrors use no room power ([Magic mirrors](#magic-mirrors)).
- Direction: the light's local +x axis rounded to a grid axis, or straight up / down with `vertical_up` / `vertical_down`.
- The beam steps one cell at a time, wraps, and passes solid voxels, Guys, Monsters, MIRRORABLE Rocks and `mirrorable` Rovers (colouring them). It stops at any Promesst_Light cell (so one lap at most, ending at its own light), a non-MIRRORABLE Rock, a non-mirrorable Rover, a Friendly_Dragon, Tree, Sign, Treasure_Display, Gem, Egg, a closed Gate (a pending GATE_OPEN or GATE_CLOSE counts), a frozen Freezer, the grid edge or z range. A Mirror turns it ([Mirrors turning fire and beams](#mirrors-turning-fire-and-beams)); the beam traces itself again from each Mirror, into the same sample list.
- Solid voxels never stop a beam: the cell lookup reads entities only. So a vertical beam leaves its layer; on the overworld a beam that starts below the map rooms ends at z 74.66 (the bottom of the lowest FAST_TRAVEL box − 0.5), so a beam turned up at z 0 climbs to z 74 unless an entity stops it.
- A beam is at infinity (`endpoint_b_is_at_infinity`) when its next sample would leave the level volume: outside the grid in x or y (never on a wrapping level), below `grid.corner.z`, above `grid.corner.z` + `squares_z`, or past the map-room cap above. `endpoint_b` is then the last cell inside the volume. On the overworld only a vertical beam can be at infinity.
- Beam powers act on whatever stands in the beam: each beam adds 1 to its colour's count on every Guy, MIRRORABLE Rock, Mirror, Rover and Monster it passes (a Rock, Mirror or Rover only when MIRRORABLE / `mirrorable`). Each light has one `color`, so ALTER comes from its own beam. The beam update retraces all beams in every settle step ([Falling](#falling)), and once more after a move's position write when a beam carrier moved.

## Mirrors turning fire and beams

- Every Mirror turns fire and beams, magic or not; `is_magic` and `mirrorable` don't matter. Facing = `theta_target` (degrees about z).
- A flat Mirror has two open sides, R(θ)·(−1, 0) and R(θ)·(0, −1): west and south at θ = 0. A ray entering through one open side leaves through the other. Output directions are rounded per axis to −1/0/+1.
- `is_vertical` without `is_vertical_downward`: open sides R(θ)·(+1, 0) and up. At θ 0 a ray moving west turns up and a ray moving down turns east; at θ 180 a ray moving east turns up, a ray moving down turns west and a ray moving west hits the back. With `is_vertical_downward`: R(θ)·(−1, 0) and down. At θ 0 a ray moving east turns down and a ray moving up turns west; at θ 90 the open sides are south and down, so a rising ray turns south.
- The back or a closed side ends the ray: fire counts it as a hit; a beam is blocked, and a `mirrorable` Mirror is coloured. DEAD Mirrors are ignored.

## Magic mirrors

- A magic Mirror's arms are its two open sides ([Mirrors turning fire and beams](#mirrors-turning-fire-and-beams)). It casts rays along both arms, up to max(W, H, 100) cells, and keeps the nearer target. Targets: Guys, Monsters, MIRRORABLE Rocks (not a SKIPPING one flagged CANNOT_MIRROR_TELEPORT_UNTIL_ANOTHER_SKIP_HAPPENS), `mirrorable` Rovers and Mirrors. The ray ends at a Sign, Tree, Gem, Promesst_Light, a Rover or Mirror that is not `mirrorable`, a non-MIRRORABLE Rock, a frozen Freezer, or a Gate whose `open` is false when the ray is cast (a pending Gate move is ignored). An open Gate does not stop it.
- The mirage (`Mirage.target_position`) stands as far behind the Mirror along the reflected path as the target stands in front: d cells from the target back to the Mirror, turned by its open sides, then d cells out the other arm.
- Solid cells use the voxel solid query ([Support](#support)). The ray stops at the first solid voxel on the target arm and on the landing leg, and the landing cell is tested again. There is no THROUGH exception: a landing inside a wall is never accepted.
- ACTIVATE gives "cannot_mirror" when any mirage is blocked: the reflection is ambiguous (two different targets at equal distance), the landing leg ends early at a solid voxel, the trace crosses a refusing edge (below), the landing cell is solid or holds a Gem, or the reflected move (MOVED_BY_MIRROR) fails the teleport move check. A mirage can't push, so that check refuses any landing-cell occupant that blocks a move: a Rock, Mirror, Guy, Monster, Rover, Tree or frozen Freezer. A floor Fence in the landing cell is accepted.
- The teleport is an ordinary move. When it finishes, the support check runs on the landing, so an unsupported landing falls ([Falling](#falling)), then the drown check: a Guy or Monster that lands at the surface or above starts a fall and drowns when it finishes. The drown on a landing 1 or 2 cells under a surface is not checked.
- Refusing edges: a Wardrobe door edge, anywhere, in a level with Wardrobes; and a blocking Fence with `is_solid_wall` on the crossed edge (`is_floor` for a vertical step), tested only when the ray starts inside a FAST_TRAVEL `Camera_Marker` box, so only in the map rooms. Other Fences never refuse.
- A mirage is processed only while the Mirror's room holds a Guy of the active hero (in a map room, the whole map-room layer). Magic Mirrors read no room power.
- ACTIVATE teleports all reflections at once only if every active mirage is OK; otherwise nothing moves. A magic Mirror whose rays find no target adds no mirage, so it spoils no other activation; a hero on a ray of that Mirror is its target as well.
- Two OK mirages with `target_position`s within 0.5 (wrap-aware) cancel the activation unless they match: the same reflected entity in two mirrors is a CLONE (it appears at both); two matching entities on one target are CLONE_UNIFY (they merge). Two Rocks match when their MIRRORABLE and SKIPPABLE bits agree; others need the same type and `mesh`; two Guys must also be the same hero carrying the same number of Gems.
- Clones: Mirrors are processed in the level's Mirror list order. The first Mirror whose mirage reflects an entity moves the original (it keeps its Pid); each later one copies it, with its Gems. Registering an entity appends it to its type's list; destroying one moves the last entry into its place.
- Facing: a flat Mirror's facing is its bisector (θ − 135°) put through the mirage's transform, plus 135°, so its open sides go to the images of its old ones. A vertical Mirror keeps its facing. Every other type's θ goes through the transform, as a quarter turn. So a reflected Guy or Monster faces the reflected way.
- A teleport can send a skipping stone on. A stone that lands unsupported in water gets CANNOT_MIRROR_TELEPORT_UNTIL_ANOTHER_SKIP_HAPPENS. A SKIPPABLE Rock takes its new `move_theta` from the reflected heading, so it skips on in the reflected direction:
    - The first Mirror on the path swaps its open sides a = R(θ)·(−1, 0) and b = R(θ)·(0, −1): heading in toward the Mirror (−a) becomes −b, heading out (+a) becomes +b, and ±b becomes ±a.
    - Each further Mirror adds the quarter turn the ray itself makes there: −90° (+x → −y) entering through side a and leaving through b, +90° entering through b.
    - A FALLING stone that is not SKIPPING uses the image of straight down instead.
- A skipping stone passing over a Mirror smashes it instead ([Skipping stones](#skipping-stones)).

## Rovers

- A Rover shuttles one square at a time along its facing axis and flips `forward` at an obstacle. Each step saves `base.position`, `visual_position` and `forward`.
- The level has one rover timer, `Settings.time_per_rover_update` (the overworld has 1.0 s). It runs down in real time, not turns, and only while the level has Rovers. At 0 every Rover steps at once (IS_ROVER_TRANSACTION), wherever the hero is. Undo and load restore the timer from the newest undo record.
- Only some overworld Rovers move, each on a straight track of a few squares, most in the sky and the underworld. The others are stuck.
- A Rover that meets an obstacle reverses along the same axis (180°). The axis is its `theta_target`: an even quarter turn runs it east-west, an odd one north-south.
- A Rover pushed into water sinks to surface − 1 ([Objects in water](#objects-in-water)) and keeps shuttling under the water, reversing at a solid voxel or an object in the next square. It carries the hero standing on it along the track, and he can step off onto any ground that borders the track. A push stalls it for two steps, then it runs on with `forward` flipped.
- What stands on a Rover rides along; one MINOR pad in the underworld rides a Rover. A Rover pushes what is in front of it. The hero can push a Rover.

## Lilies

- Lilies drift in real time, one move transaction per `movement_group` (`hs_ika`, `lower_falls`, `mixup`, `wizard_nw`, `upper_falls`; `nonmoving` in the map rooms), which renews itself when it finishes. They keep drifting while the hero stands still.
- A group steps every 2 × `Settings.time_per_lily_update` (type default 0.4 s); the lily's position is written halfway through. Lily transactions never block the player ([A move](#a-move)).
- Direction per step: `kick_direction` if kicked; else the flow under the lily, when water is there and the lily is at most 0.5 above the surface; else its last `moving_direction` (it coasts across still water). Lilies within 0.1 of a rewritten river square take the new direction. `moving_direction` never returns to NONE in the saves.
- A lily's step is blocked by: no water at its level with something solid under the target, a solid voxel, a Rock, Judge, Promesst_Light, Gem, Rover, Tree, Sign or Treasure_Display, a closed Gate or frozen Freezer, a Mirror, Monster or Friendly_Dragon, another lily. An entity in the target cell whose supporter chain ends at a Lily is skipped, so a rider never blocks its own lily; so is a protected Guy. With no water at the lily's level and nothing solid under the target, the lily moves off the edge and falls.
- Riding: a Lily in an entity's own cell supports it, so a hero or rock boards by walking or falling into the lily's cell. Every entity supported by a mover, riders of riders too, gets the mover's delta as a SUPPORT move, for lily, skip, push, elevator and similar moves. A fall carries its riders by starting their own falls ([Falling](#falling)). A rider whose own move fails stays and loses its support. Riders leave the map with the lily.
- Stepping off: input is never gated while riding. The hero's step starts from his current cell and goes through the normal move check; it resolves just before the lily's own move, and the lily drifts on without him.
- Kicks come only from skipping stones: a SKIPPING stone that comes to rest supported by a Lily kicks it along the stone's `move_theta`; a stone skipping over water kicks the lilies in the cells beside its cell, on either side of its heading, away from the stone's lane ([Skipping stones](#skipping-stones)). A lily on the lane is not pushed this way: it stops the stone. A kick on a lily mid-step re-targets it at once (a blocked kick leaves it still); otherwise it sets `kick_direction`, and a kick opposite the pending one cancels it. The next step takes the kick after `Settings.lily_kickoff_time` has passed, then clears it. The hero never kicks a lily.
- On a level with a `diagonal_push_okay` Marker (only `skipping_pals`), a hero riding a Lily who steps toward an oncoming Lily whose rider stands in the marker can push that rider. Without the marker the step is blocked.
- When the hero rides a drifting Lily and steps toward an oncoming Lily (its step is opposite to his Lily's and it arrives at his target), whatever rides that Lily is given the hero's step and put through the move check without pushes, so SMASH can apply to it.

## Rivers

- A river is the flow `Direction` in water squares ([Water](#water)). Several overworld maps hold rivers. The level data has no `Flow_Updater`; a Flow_Updater is a short-lived front created when a river is blocked or freed.
- A river square is blocked when one of these settles in it with its top at the surface (entity z = surface − 1): Rock, Judge, Promesst_Light, Gem, Rover, Tree, Sign, Treasure_Display, Mirror, a closed Gate or frozen Freezer. Guys, Monsters, Lilies and Eggs never block. Deeper sunk rocks don't block. GATE_CLOSE / GATE_OPEN block and free.
- Blocking spawns an `ERASING` front walking downstream setting direction 0 (the water stays), stopping where another current still feeds the square, at a block or at a `stop_flow_erasure` Marker; and, for each upstream neighbour that flowed in, a diverting front by its `river_polarity` (TURNS_RIGHT → right). It turns the river 90° that way if that side has water at the surface, no blocker and no flow back; else the other side; else it gives up. Once turned, the polarity becomes the side taken and the front keeps writing its direction into still water until it meets flowing water (merge), no water, or a block (it diverts again). A flat Mirror blocker picks the side itself when the flow enters through one of its open sides: the front turns toward the other open side. A flow that hits its back, and a vertical Mirror, use the polarity rule.
- Freeing a square lets the upstream current back in and a FLOWING_FORWARD front refills the erased channel. Diverted squares keep their new flow, so the result depends on history.
- A Flow_Updater only rewrites squares that already hold water: `ocean_values[idx] = 1 | (river_polarity == 1) << 1 | direction << 2`.

## Elevators

- An elevator is a `Stairs` frame pair (`other_end_id`, `platform_id`) and one `Elevator_Platform` (`bottom_owner`, `top_owner`, `seconds_per_square`). No elevator is one-way by design. The platform's state is its position; in the level data most rest at the bottom, the rest at the top.
- A Guy moving into the platform's cell starts it (the frame does not). It moves one square per step as a normal move, never into a solid voxel, until within 0.5 of the far frame. The lower half of the shaft goes up, the upper half down; it goes down unless a blocker is directly below.
- Up: refused with `elevator_too_heavy` when the stack riding it counts 2 or more, by the push weight's count ([Pushing and pulling](#pushing-and-pulling)). The hero counts, so he rides up alone and never with a Rock. Also refused by a non-following Friendly_Dragon directly above or by the move check.
- Down: no weight limit. A step is refused by a Rock, Judge, Promesst_Light, Gem, Rover, Tree, Sign, Treasure_Display, closed Gate, frozen Freezer, Guy, Monster or non-following Friendly_Dragon in the next cell. A Mirror there is smashed.
- Calling: moving into a `Stairs` cell whose platform is idle and more than 0.1 away calls it there under the same blocking rules; the hero doesn't move. A blocking non-floor Fence on that edge stops the call. The call has no weight check, so a platform can be called up with a Rock on it.
- A platform is never stuck, and a step tests only the next cell. A platform resting in a frozen Freezer's cell leaves it when called up but can't come back down into it, and the hero can't enter that cell, so such an elevator links nothing while the Freezer is closed. Some overworld elevators have their platform in the frozen Freezer of their bottom frame cell.
- Per elevator (level data): one links nothing (a crystal stands on its platform in a cage fenced on four sides); the map-room copy of the hub elevator works bottom → top only; the hub elevator clears only from the top ([Routes that need these mechanics](#routes-that-need-these-mechanics)); those in frozen Freezers are blocked while their Freezer is closed; the rest work both ways.

## Teleporter pads and fast travel

- A pad sends a controlled Guy who stands on it to its `target_id` when `enabled_t_target` ≥ 0.5. The pad check runs only on the TakeOrDrop input (the one that enters a level), over the controlled Guys only. A step, a bump, a fall or ACTIVATE never fires a pad, so a hero walks across an enabled pad without being sent. Pushed objects on a pad stay where they are.
- The target is any entity (no type check); the hero goes to its position (wrapped) by a TELEPORT move that is added only if the move check passes, so a target cell holding a Tree, Sign, Judge, Treasure_Display or frozen Freezer keeps the hero on the pad.
- A world MAJOR pad's target is its twin in a map room, and the twin's target is the world pad. Fast travel is: take a pad, walk in the map room to an enabled twin, take it. There is no direct world-to-world jump.
- Many MINOR pads target a Marker, not a pad, mostly in the sky and the underworld; the hero lands at the Marker and falls from there. A Marker target may stand far above the pad, so such a pad can lift the hero.
- Stepping on a pad discovers it. `discover(T)` does nothing when `enabled_t_target` = 0. Else it sets `discovered`, then enables and discovers `target_id`, enables `also_discover`, its target, and discovers it with chaining, and enables `also_discover2` and sets `discovered` on it without chaining.
- `enable(T)` sets `enabled_t_target` = 1. For a MAJOR pad it also makes the pad's `group_id` and `also_exist_this_group_id` Groups exist when they are existence groups and do not exist.
- Every `also_discover` link sits on a map-room twin and names another twin: discovering a twin enables the twins it names and their world pads and makes their Groups exist.
- The MAJOR pads of the sky antipode hub (the sky towers' pads, [Second Endgame](progression.md#second-endgame)) point `also_exist_this_group_id` at Group `FTEnd2` (FAST_TRAVEL_SKY, `exists_by_default` 0, in the sky map room). They are the targets of the sky map room's twins.
- Fast-travel Markers act on touch (on the overworld all in the world): `FastTravelEnableGroup` makes its target Group exist (those at the hub enable `FTEnd1`; the one in the ruby lift's landing cell enables `FastTravelVerticalAccess`, the map room's copy of the lift, [Routes that need these mechanics](#routes-that-need-these-mechanics)); `FastTravelDiscoverTeleporter` discovers its target pad (one, in the same landing cell); `SmashFastTravelRock`, on the hub elevator's top cell, when the hero stands still on it, destroys `ruby_sky_fast_travel_elevator_rock`, the rock on the map-room copy of that elevator, making it usable.
- Gold rooms: the ground Towers' `teleporter_id` are MINOR pads (disabled) next to each tower. Their targets are the `gold_room_endpoint` pads in the ground antipode hub, one per tower, half a world period from the crossroads ([Endgame](progression.md#endgame)). Tower activation enables the pad.

## Activators and Spawners

- ACTIVATE finds an Activator in the hero's own cell and runs each of its Spawners. A Spawner destroys the entities whose `transmute_spawner_source_id` points at it and the entity holding `spawned_entity_id`, then clones `prototype_entity_to_clone` with the id `spawned_entity_id` (the Pid is reused). Respawns happen only on ACTIVATE; a level load builds the prototypes but spawns nothing.
- An Ocean_Map Spawner (`ocean_resetter` set) copies its prototype's `ocean_values` into the live map and kills that map's Flow_Updaters. That can clear bit 0: water can drain again.
- A Spawner's clone appears at the Spawner's own cell, one instance at a time. The respawn is skipped while the Spawner's cell is blocked, and the old instance then stays. The clone keeps the prototype's movement group. The Spawner sets no Lily direction field, so the clone's first step follows the ordinary rule ([Lilies](#lilies)): a clone on a river drifts with the flow under the Spawner's cell, and a clone on still water does not move.
- A Lily Spawner usually stands in the Lily's own cell; seven stand along one underworld river row, each beside its own Activator, for one Lily.
- A Rock's Spawner almost always stands in the Rock's own starting cell, so a reset puts the Rock back where it started, wherever it was pushed, and also destroys a Rock that sank in water. That takes the bridge back ([Objects in water](#objects-in-water)): the water square is unchanged, so the cell is open again and the new Rock can be pushed in again. A hero on the bridge then falls into the water (inferred).

## Routes that need these mechanics

Ways into parts of the overworld that a walk on foot with single pushes doesn't find. Each follows from the rules above.

- The hub elevator (80,69,−21) ↔ (80,69,33). The platform rests at the top under a 3-rock stack (`ruby_sky_elevator_rock` z 33, `ruby_sky_elevator_crystal` z 34, `ruby_sky_elevator_stone` z 35); the top cell is fenced east, north and west, the shaft cell (80,69,0) on all four sides. The bottom Stairs cell is unfenced, with a PULL Wardrobe at (76,70,−21). Calling the platform down brings the stack (inferred): the bottom Rock then carries two, weight 3, beyond SINGLE_PUSH. So the stack clears only from the top: PULL from (80,68,33) to (80,67,33), or a TELEPORT swap. The map rooms copy the shaft 1:1 at (62,120,89). `SmashFastTravelRock` fires when the hero stands still on its Marker at (80,69,33) ([Teleporter pads and fast travel](#teleporter-pads-and-fast-travel)).
