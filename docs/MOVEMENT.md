# Movement

How a move resolves (support, falling, water, pushing and pulling) and every mechanic that moves things: gates, rocks,
skipping stones, monsters, beams, mirrors, rovers, lilies, rivers, elevators, pads and Spawners.

## A move

- A key press queues a move: a per-level input queue of at most 6 requests, extra presses dropped, run one at a time. ACTIVATE goes into the same queue as a request with no direction. The frame update takes the next move only when no in-flight transaction is BLOCKING_PLAYER (except one flagged BLOCKING_PLAYER_ONLY_UNTIL_PHYSICAL_MOVE that has PHYSICALLY_MOVED) and the held-keys transaction is absent or DONE.
- Every hero move transaction is created BLOCKING_PLAYER, so presses wait until the hero's own step is DONE. Lily, Rover and falling transactions from them don't block the player. Elevator moves do.
- A move heading into the hero's target cell that has not physically moved yet, by a Rock, Rover, Lily, Guy or a few other types, marks its transaction BLOCKING_PLAYER plus BLOCKING_PLAYER_ONLY_UNTIL_PHYSICAL_MOVE; the press waits until it lands.
- The move check tests the destination cell for solid voxels, blocking entities and fences on the edge (start cell side and destination side). It does not test cells in between (DOUBLE and ENDPOINT moves skip cells). The fence-edge test is skipped for a TELEPORT move and for a mover with SQUEEZE, so a pad or a magic-mirror teleport lands across a Fence; the Wardrobe door test is not skipped.
- Occupants that never block a move: Door, Switch, Rubble, Flat_Decoration, Lily, Elevator_Platform, Gem_Slot, Teleporter, Level_Entry, an unfrozen Freezer and a DEAD Monster. So a pushed Rock enters a Level_Entry's cell as it enters a Switch's.
- The move code runs the move check from seven places: the TELEPORT and ENDPOINT-jump check, without pushes; the normal step, with pushes; PULL; a hero on a drifting Lily stepping toward an oncoming Lily ([Lilies](#lilies)); and three for CHARM: each charmed thing, a charmed thing following its supporter's path, and the Bard's own move with a push list.
- A hero inside a solid voxel, a Tree's cell or a frozen Freezer's cell, outside a THROUGH beam, is stuck and cannot move.
- No climbing: the hero never steps up. There is no fall damage.
- Timing: when a transaction closes, its duration is the longest of its moves. Its physical time is now plus: for a hero step, the step's own value below; otherwise the smallest non-negative `duration_until_physical_move` of its moves, else half the duration; capped at the duration. Turn processing writes the positions when level time reaches the physical time, and the transaction is DONE when its elapsed time reaches the duration.
- Move durations are constants, not Settings fields:
  - the hero's step (CONTROLLER_PROC): max(0.21, 0.07) + 0.5 × the current Animation_Node's `move_accel_time_override` (0.2 when negative), so 0.31 s by default. Its positions are written 0.16 s / run speed after the transaction closes. Run speed is a per-level value that ramps from 1.0 toward 2.3 while input is queued (inferred);
  - a pushed entity: 0.15 s, its own move from the power solver. In a DOUBLE beam it is a TELEPORT of 0.18 s, 0.17 s for a SKIPPING Rock. A row adds no time: all pushes are in the hero's transaction, written together at his physical time, and the transaction ends with his 0.31 s step;
  - a magic-mirror teleport: a TELEPORT flagged MOVED_BY_MIRROR, 0.2 s, positions written at 0.1 s;
  - a wizard swap, a teleporter pad and the Rod: 0.28 s. TRANSMUTE: 0.38 s. The item-use STATIONARY move: 0.15 s. A bump: a 0.15 s STATIONARY move; a cancelled charm: 0.6 s;
  - falls, skips and lily steps: [Falling](#falling), [Skipping stones](#skipping-stones), [Lilies](#lilies).

## Support

An entity with DOES_NOT_FALL in `entity_flags` is always supported: the support check returns at once, and the support
update, the fall test and the turn's fall list all skip it. Any other entity is supported when the support check finds
any of these (the `protected` fallback runs before the water and implicit-floor tests):

- its own cell is solid: it is stuck in a wall (the solid query, below). This is the check's first test, and it holds over water and in mid-air alike, so an entity inside a solid voxel never falls or sinks out of it. Guys are held this way too; static types and a few flagged ones are exempt;
- a solid voxel below;
- in the probe cell (x, y, z − d + 1), where d is the drop: 1 normally, so the entity's own cell, or the DOUBLE multiplier in a powered fall. There a Lily (for every non-Lily entity), an Elevator_Platform or a Fence with `blocking` and `is_floor` supports for any d, and a Tree only when d < 1.1, so never in a DOUBLE fall;
- below it: any solid-like entity (Rock, Sign, Tree, Gem, Rover, Judge, Promesst_Light, Treasure_Display), a closed Gate (`open` false counts as solid, unless the Gate is FLAT), a frozen Freezer, a Monster, a Guy, the Friendly_Dragon or an `is_vertical` Mirror. A Promesst_Light block supports; its beam never does;
- for a Guy with `protected`: supported anywhere, over air too (last fallback, no position test);
- the implicit floor. Dry square: supported at z ≤ the layer base, in the ground layer and the map rooms only (in a world with an Annihilator this applies only on OVERWORLD and not underground). So the overworld has a dry floor at z ≤ 0 on the ground and in the map rooms, none underground or in the sky. Water square: supported only below the ground floor: at z < `ground_z0` outside the underworld band (from `underground_z0` up to `underground_barrier`), and at z < `underground_z0`. On the overworld that is z −4..−1 and z < −25. Water at or above `ground_z0` holds nothing up, in the sky and the map rooms alike; `skyground_z0` plays no part. A square of any Ocean_Map, PLAYER_BLOCKING_ONLY included, is a water square here: such a map only switches off the dry floor. So nothing stands on a PLAYER_BLOCKING_ONLY map's surface: a Rock pushed over it falls through, and the hero's step onto it must pass the step's water check ([Water](#water)).

Supporters the check passes over: the entity itself, DEAD entities, anything `scheduled_for_destruction`, FALLING entities (unless the caller allows them), and a Gem under a TAKE_GEMS Guy. There is no type filter on the supported entity: every entity that runs the check is held the same way. So a TAKE_GEMS Guy over a Gem falls
into its cell (the move check lets him in, [Pushing and pulling](#pushing-and-pulling)), and a dead body holds nothing
up. A kill does not check again what the body held up: support and falls are checked when an entity's own move
finishes, and for everything at once by the level-wide sweep: a support check of every entity, then a fall for each
unsupported one, lowest first. The sweep runs when a level is freshly loaded (for the overworld, its first load in a
session), when a level is restored to its start state, and once at the story's first ending. It does not run after an
ordinary undo or when the hero comes back to the overworld from a puzzle level, which stays loaded: the return runs no support sweep and no fall or skip check. The level-change routine, which runs for a fresh load and a return alike, destroys at once every Level_Entry whose `currently_broken` or `currently_disfavored` is set, starts the sound and visual instances of the Guys, Rovers and Promesst_Lights again, registers every Lily that has a `moving_direction` with its movement group again, and posts the level-load sound events. Only a fresh load (the overworld's first load) goes on to the load and sweep steps.
So a Mirror on a goblin that dies stays where it is until it moves or until the overworld's next fresh load, when it
falls.

The support check does not look at a vertical ENDPOINT leg: a Guy in such a leg is unsupported. It is the fall that fails ([Promesst beams and powers](#promesst-beams-and-powers)).

In a DOUBLE beam d is the fall's multiplier and the check looks that many cells down ([Promesst beams and powers](#promesst-beams-and-powers)).

The solid query reads the voxels and the level's column mask. A column is outside the level when it has no WALLS
Wall_Set voxel, no Ocean_Map square and no floor Fence (`is_floor`). A voxel above or below the grid's z range loses
its own bit but still puts its column inside ([The grid](GAMEDATA.md#the-grid)). In a level with an Annihilator every
column counts as inside. A cell in the grid whose column is outside the level is solid at every z. A cell outside the
grid is solid only below `grid.corner.z`. The grid is `Settings.grid` as the load leaves it ([The grid](GAMEDATA.md#the-grid)).
An entity whose own cell is solid this way counts as stuck in a wall, and the support check holds it there.

## Falling

- An unsupported entity falls one cell per step until it meets support. No height limit, no damage to the faller. Each step is one FALLING move of one cell down and takes 0.1 / `Settings.falling_rate` s. Beam powers can turn a step into a jump: a DOUBLE beam scales it, and an ENDPOINT beam parallel to the fall (any leg that points up) turns it into a jump to `endpoint_a`, which a blocked landing cancels ([Promesst beams and powers](#promesst-beams-and-powers)).
- In a water square the fall stops at surface − 1 (the Ocean_Map's z − 1), on every layer and at any depth: a step is cancelled when the next z satisfies `surface − 3 < tz < surface − 1`. The water here is a non-PLAYER_BLOCKING_ONLY map in tz's layer with map z ≤ tz + 2.1.
- A Lily is supported in water while z ≤ surface + 0.5: it floats at the surface. Only Lilies float, and never on a PLAYER_BLOCKING_ONLY map.
- A protected Guy never falls.
- Order in a turn, once the move's positions are written. This settle step runs for every move transaction: the player's move, and each later one (falls, kills, Gate moves). An ACTIVATE input is itself the move, so the magic-mirror teleport is written before that settle's protection and attack passes ([Magic mirrors](#magic-mirrors)). The steps: the protection pass; the switch and Gate updates; the beam update; the support check of everything that moved, which puts the unsupported on a fall list; the attack pass (goblin attacks and fire: `protected` set for every hit, then the kills); the switch update again if anything changed, then the monster Gate update; the magic-mirror mirage and circle updates, which make no moves; a second protection pass (no copy into `was_protected`) and a second attack pass, which kills nothing and only turns hits on `pre_protected` Guys into `protected`; the support check of every Guy whose `protected` just cleared; then the falls of the fall list start, lowest first, leaving out entities already in a move and DEAD ones. So a fall reads `protected` as the attack passes leave it: a hero who walks into water and is hit that turn stays at the surface, and a Mirror pushed into water still turns fire that turn before it sinks ([Goblins, dragons and PROTECTION](#goblins-dragons-and-protection)).
- The level-wide support sweep (at a level load) runs the same checks but starts its falls itself ([Support](#support)).
- Below the Annihilator's z (−100) a fall step becomes an annihilation.
- A falling Guy or Monster lands on a Guy or Monster below it. A falling solid entity (Rock, Gem, Promesst_Light, Rover, Tree, ...) enters the cell and kills the Guy or Monster there, unless the fall is short (`z_when_falling_started` under 2.1 above the target) and that one moves away in the same move. A falling entity smashes a flat Mirror it lands on.
- On the overworld's ground and sky a Guy may step off any dry ledge into air, with no height limit; he falls to the first support (on the ground the implicit floor at z ≤ 0; the sky has none). On a dry square the move check refuses a ledge step in two cases only. In the UNDERGROUND layer of an OVERWORLD level the step needs support somewhere below, down to `underground_z0` − 1.1 (−26.1), or a matching jump marker; otherwise the hero would fall to the Annihilator. In a level with `Settings.prevent_walking_into_annihilator` it needs support down to z −0.1 (set in a few non-overworld levels; false on the overworld). Layer bands are no barrier: they only pick the implicit floor and the underground rule.
- A lily drop is a FALLING (3) move; riders follow as SUPPORT moves. Leaving the grid sets DISAPPEAR_DUE_TO_LEAVING_THE_MAP.

## Water

- Water query: the water list is the existing Ocean_Maps sorted by z, highest first. A lookup takes the first map with bit 0 of `ocean_values` at the square, in the position's ground layer, with map z ≤ pos.z + 2.1. `PLAYER_BLOCKING_ONLY` maps count only when the caller asks for them: lily floating, drowning and the fall's water landing skip them; the support check's water test and the hero's step water check include them. So such a map holds no one up and lets nothing float, but the hero steps onto it only where the water step check below passes. The overworld's such maps are all in the map rooms. In prelude levels they can cover the holes in a prelude room's floor ([Existence groups](ENTITIES.md#existence-groups)).
- `ocean_values` per square: bit 0 water, bit 1 `River_Polarity` (0 TURNS_RIGHT, 1 TURNS_LEFT), bits 2-4 flow `Direction` (1 E, 2 N, 3 W, 4 S). Square index `(x − corner.x) + (y − corner.y) × squares_x` in the map's local grid, which the map's orientation turns about its position ([Ocean_Map (water)](GAMEDATA.md#ocean_map-water)), x/y rounded with +0.5 floor, wrapped.
- `detect_holes`: every square starts with bit 0, then each voxel of an `OCEAN_HOLE` Wall_Set with `ocean_hole_id` = the map's pid clears it.
- Drowning: after each move settles, a Guy or Monster whose water surface (found from z + 2, non-PLAYER_BLOCKING_ONLY map) is in [z + 0.5, z + 2.5] dies (StateDrown, `status_flags` DEAD), unless its `supported_by_id` is an Elevator_Platform. That is 1 or 2 cells under the surface. Deeper water is invisible to the move, fall and drown checks. PROTECTION doesn't prevent drowning.
- So: a Guy that falls into water ends at surface − 1 and drowns. A Monster pushed into water drowns (Monsters skip the water move check). A Rock pushed in becomes a bridge with its top at the surface, on every layer.
- Stepping into water (Guys and Rovers; Monsters skip it). For a Guy that isn't protected the step check walks the cells from the target z down to surface − 0.1, one cell at a time, and runs the full support check ([Support](#support)) on the hero at each. The first cell that passes accepts the step. So a floor Fence in the target cell at or above surface − 0.1 accepts it, a submerged one deeper than that is never reached, and a target inside a solid voxel (a THROUGH step) accepts it. The step is allowed when any of these holds:
  - support at some z′ in that range;
  - for such a z′, in the same loop, the cell is attacked (a goblin within 1.1, or on a dragon's traced segment) and the hero has PROTECTION, `pre_protected` or `protected`, or a PROTECTION Guy is within 1.1;
  - a Mirror or Gem in the cell below z′ (map not PLAYER_BLOCKING_ONLY), tested in the same loop;
  - a Promesst_Light in the cell under the target;
  - another entity's pending move (a solid entity or Mirror) ending within 0.5 horizontally of the target with z in [surface − 1.1, target z + 0.1);
  - the hero rides a supporter moved by another transaction;
  - the hero stands at the surface z and a powered DOUBLE beam's position sample is within 0.1 of the target.
- A protected Guy may always step into water, except in the UNDERGROUND layer of an OVERWORLD level. In a level with `Settings.prevent_walking_into_annihilator` the step must pass that check as well ([Falling](#falling)). The step check reads `protected` as the previous turn processing left it ([Goblins, dragons and PROTECTION](#goblins-dragons-and-protection)).
- A `may_jump_into_water` Marker (on the overworld, at sky and underworld edges): at the hero's position, with the move direction · its listed direction ≥ 0.5, it allows the step outright only when the hero is more than 0.5 above the surface and the map is not INVISIBLE. The hero then falls to surface − 1 and drowns. Every overworld jump marker is a death edge, never a route.
- Merged undo records keep OCEAN_CHANGE actions; a river is never drained, only redirected ([Rivers](#rivers)). Only `ocean_resetter` Spawners can take water away again ([Activators and Spawners](#activators-and-spawners)).

## Pushing and pulling

- Walking into a Rock, Guy, Monster, Mirror, Gem, Rover, Promesst_Light or Friendly_Dragon pushes it one cell, and it pushes whatever is in its own way. Trees, Signs, Judges, Treasure_Displays and walls are never pushed: they block. The move check skips DEAD occupants, and it builds the push in the same loop, so a dead body neither blocks nor is pushed.
- Push strength s = STRONG beam count + (MULTIPUSH ? 2 : SINGLE_PUSH ? 1 : 0). s ≥ 1 can push, s ≥ 2 can multipush. STRONG + ALTER cancels pushing and gives PULL. STRONG + SINGLE_PUSH works like MULTIPUSH.
- Weight W: each pushed thing counts 1, plus 1 for everything stacked on it, found through what supports what, level by level. Not counted: Switch, Lily, Artifact, Door, Gate, Rubble, dead Guys and Monsters, Flat_Decoration, Level_Entry, Elevator_Platform, Gem_Slot, Teleporter, an unfrozen Freezer, FLAT entities, anything `scheduled_for_destruction`. A following Friendly_Dragon (`following_id` ≠ 0) ends the whole count wherever it is reached. W ≥ 1 needs s ≥ 1, W ≥ 2 needs s ≥ 2; otherwise the move is a bump ("no_multipush"). The same count decides the weight crush below and `elevator_too_heavy` ([Elevators](#elevators)).
- So SINGLE_PUSH moves exactly one object with nothing countable on top, and MULTIPUSH moves a row and stacks of any length (no cap found). A Wardrobe skill replaces SINGLE_PUSH, so a PULL, TELEPORT or PROTECTION hero can't push ([Wardrobes](SKILLS.md#wardrobes)).
- Distance: one cell. A pusher's step longer than 1.1 (DOUBLE, a jump) is cut to one cell per axis for the push. Then the push goes through the power solver with the pushed entity's own colours (a Guy, MIRRORABLE Rock, `mirrorable` Mirror or Rover, a Monster), so its own DOUBLE beams scale it.
- Weight crush: tested once per turn, in turn processing after the end-of-turn updates, on three kinds only: Guys without MULTIPUSH, Rovers and Monsters. Rocks and Mirrors are never crushed, so a Rock under a stack never becomes Rubble by weight. Entities that don't exist ([Existence groups](ENTITIES.md#existence-groups)), and PLAYABLE_IN_PRELUDE ones outside the prelude, are skipped.
  - Crush strength s: a Guy has STRONG + 1 with SINGLE_PUSH, else STRONG. A Monster has STRONG + 1. A `mirrorable` Rover has STRONG + 1; a plain Rover has 1. STRONG and ALTER together give s = 0. s ≥ 2 is never crushed, so one STRONG beam makes a Monster or a `mirrorable` Rover immune. Crush strength is not push strength: MULTIPUSH plays no part.
  - Weight W is the entity itself plus everything it supports, counted as for the push weight above.
  - It is crushed when W − 1 > s: when the countable things above it outnumber its strength. So a SINGLE_PUSH Guy dies under 2 or more, and a Guy with no push skill (a PULL, TELEPORT or PROTECTION hero, [Wardrobes](SKILLS.md#wardrobes)) under 1.
  - A crushed Rover is destroyed at once and Rubble is created in its place (`rubble_type` ROVER, "rover_smash"). A crushed Guy or Monster dies the smash death at once (DEAD, "goblin_smash" / "gargoyle_smash"), with a STATIONARY move when it is in no transaction.
  - What rested on it falls unless something else holds it, as after any other loss of support. Before the kill or smash the test collects every entity the crushed one directly supports. After the three crush passes (Guys, Rovers, Monsters) turn processing reruns the support update on that list. A dead body holds nothing up and Rubble is no supporter, so each one is either held by something else (it may join a moving supporter's transaction as a rider) or goes on the turn's fall list. That list drops entities with DOES_NOT_FALL, entities already in a transaction and a few excluded types; the rest start an ordinary fall ([Falling](#falling)), and their own riders follow as SUPPORT riders. The fall pass runs in the move-finish call of turn processing, every play turn, not in the level-start call.
- A push fails, and the hero bumps, if any pushed thing can't make its own move: a solid voxel at its target (unless that thing is in a THROUGH beam), a fence edge, a Tree, Sign, Judge, Treasure_Display, frozen Freezer or closed Gate in the next cell, a pushable that can't move, the row looping back on itself or to the pusher (wrap), or the pushed thing stuck in a wall. One failure blocks the whole push. So a Rock in a T-junction reached from the stem can't be pushed into the wall.
- A refused step, blocked or refused by weight, runs a bump over its push list: each entry that doesn't move gets its pusher's SMASH or OPEN effect ([Promesst beams and powers](#promesst-beams-and-powers)). Entries that move get push moves and are never bumped.
- A TAKE_GEMS Guy is not blocked by Gems: the move check skips a Gem occupant for him, so he walks or falls into its cell. A Friendly_Dragon that follows the mover swaps places with him instead (`SWAP_WITH_DRAGON`).
- PULL: [TELEPORT and PULL](SKILLS.md#teleport-and-pull). A pushed object falls when unsupported ([Falling](#falling), [Objects in water](#objects-in-water)).

## Gates, switches and buttons

- A Gate blocks its cell while closed, and a closed Gate that is not FLAT holds up what stands in the cell above ([Support](#support)). Under water at surface − 1 it is a bridge: the hero stands on it at the surface and does not drown, and the step into water passes, since the support lies between the target z and surface − 0.1. An open Gate holds up nothing, so a skipping stone passes over it.
- A switch gate is open when every existing Switch of its `flavor` is pressed, xor `notted`. So pressing every Switch of a flavor opens its plain gates and shuts its notted ones together. The switch update leaves alone a gate with no existing Switch of its `flavor` (every glyph gate, and a few other overworld gates): whatever opens or shuts it stays.
- A Switch is pressed by a Tree, Rock, Monster, Promesst_Light, Mirror, Artifact, Gem or Rover in its cell. A Guy presses only `special_type` NORMAL; END1, END3_ROCK_DETERMINED and ENDGAME_IGNORE_PEOPLE ignore Guys. A closed Gate in its cell holds a NORMAL Switch down. Every overworld Switch is NORMAL.
- The press goes through the cell lookup, so only what is on the grid presses. A Gem carried by a TAKE_GEMS Guy is off the grid ([TRANSMUTE and the other skills](SKILLS.md#transmute-and-the-other-skills)): a hero who takes the Gem from a Switch leaves it empty when he steps off.
- A Switch is pressed only while something stands in it. The overworld has one Guy ([Entity catalogue](ENTITIES.md#entity-catalogue)), so the hero can hold one Switch, but not while he walks through its Gate, and never two at once. A switch gate the hero must pass, or a flavor with more than one Switch, needs an object left on each Switch.
- Gate moves (GATE_OPEN, GATE_CLOSE) come from four sources only: the OPEN bump, the ALTER bump, the switch update (which leaves out monster gates and flavors with no existing Switch) and the monster gate update. Load and undo restore `open` like any field. So a Gate that is no monster gate and whose flavor has no Switch opens only by an OPEN bump and shuts only by an ALTER bump.
- A monster gate (`is_monster_gate`) is open when no living Monster (no DEAD in `status_flags`) stands in its screen room, xor `notted`. The room of the Gate and of each Monster is the screen of the grid that holds `floor(x + 0.5)`, `floor(y + 0.5)` (clamped into the grid), plus a layer step chosen by z: the first layer band holding z, else the nearest band below. A Monster counts when it exists (its Group exists; a PLAYABLE_IN_PRELUDE one only during the prelude), in any state. The gate update recomputes `room_index` each time and runs once per turn, after the attack pass ([Falling](#falling)), so the gate follows its room: it opens when the room's last Monster dies (drowned, crushed, burnt or hit by a goblin) and shuts again when a living Monster is in the room again. Every Monster counts, goblins and dragons included. A pending GATE_OPEN or GATE_CLOSE counts as the gate's state now.
- A Gate move (GATE_OPEN 17 / GATE_CLOSE 18) blocks and frees river squares like a blocker ([Rivers](#rivers)).
- A closing gate acts like a one-cell move from behind into its own cell, along its local −z axis (local −x with `sideways`), reversed by `negate_direction`, rounded to a grid axis. For an upright gate that is straight down. Whatever stands in the gate's cell is pushed one cell that way (a strong push, no weight limit). If it can't move, a Guy or Monster there is killed, and a Rock, Judge, Promesst_Light, Gem, Rover, Tree, Sign, Treasure_Display, Mirror or frozen Freezer is smashed to Rubble. Another Gate is ignored. So a gate shutting on a hero who stands on a floor kills him.
- `barrier_activate` / `barrier_deactivate` are Gate force-field sound and animation events, not existence.
- OPEN: a mover in an OPEN beam and in no ALTER beam that moves into a closed Gate opens it. The mover bumps ("open_gate"; it stays and the turn is used) and the gate gets a GATE_OPEN move. The mover can be a Guy, a MIRRORABLE Rock, a `mirrorable` Mirror, a `mirrorable` Rover or a Monster, by a step, a push, a fall or a skip. With ALTER as well, moving into an open Gate shuts it ("alter_open_gate"). Either way the gate's `notted` flips with the change, so a switch gate keeps its new state until its switches change. Any Gate works, not only the glyph meshes. The beam itself opens nothing: it stops at a closed Gate.
- A Gate Spawner's `gate_resetter` decides whether the respawn treats a Gate in its cell as blocking.

## Objects in water

- Whatever is pushed off support into water falls and rests at surface − 1, at any depth and on every layer: a Rock that is not SKIPPABLE, a Gem, a Mirror (plain, magic or vertical), a Promesst_Light, a Rover or a Monster (a Monster drowns there). Only a Friendly_Dragon never starts a fall, so it never sinks.
- What then holds up the hero, or an object pushed onto the cell: a Rock, a Gem, a Promesst_Light, a Rover and a vertical Mirror (`is_vertical`). So the sunk object's top is a floor at the surface: a bridge. A second object pushed over the first sinks in the next water cell, so a gap of several water cells closes with one object per cell.
- A flat Mirror (plain or magic) holds up nothing: whatever lands in its cell from above, the hero included, falls into it, smashes it (it becomes Rubble) and ends at surface − 1 there. A dead Monster holds nothing either. A hero with TAKE_GEMS falls through a Gem, which the move check passes over for him ([Pushing and pulling](#pushing-and-pulling)).
- A sunk Gem holds the hero and objects pushed onto it; a sunk Mirror does not hold him (seen in play).
- A skipping stone that sank in place against a wall ([Skipping stones](#skipping-stones)) holds the hero like a sunk Rock, and a magic-mirror hop ([Magic mirrors](#magic-mirrors)) lands on it ([Routes that need these mechanics](#routes-that-need-these-mechanics)).
- A Rock that sits at surface − 1 in a river square blocks the river ([Rivers](#rivers)), and so do the other things that settle there. A Rock riding a Lily stands at the surface and does not.
- The sunk object holds up what stands on the cell above it ([Support](#support)). It is the only floor there: the water square keeps its value. An Activator reset of the object's Spawner destroys it, which takes the bridge back ([Activators and Spawners](#activators-and-spawners)).

## Skipping stones

- The moves that set a Rock's `move_theta` from their direction include CHARMED ones, which also count as launches. So a charmed SKIPPABLE stone that ends unsupported in level water starts skipping like a pushed one.
- A SKIPPABLE Rock starts skipping after it is moved when nothing supports it, it has a direction (`move_theta` ≠ −1), it is not skipping already, and it stands in a water square whose surface is in [rock z − 0.5, rock z + 0.5). A move flagged START_SKIPPING also starts a skip. It sets SKIPPING and `time_started_skipping`; SKIPPING stays set until a hop ends the skip. An unsupported Rock that fails any condition falls instead.
- Timing is real time, not turns. Each skip is a transaction (IS_SKIPPING_TRANSACTION) lasting `Settings.time_per_skip` (0.12 s if unset or < 0.01; the overworld has 0.24). `speed_mod` FAST_HARDCODED uses 0.16 s; SLOW_SCALED is × 1.43. Nothing sets the skip's physical time, so the stone's `base.position` is written halfway, at `time_per_skip` / 2 ([A move](#a-move)). The skip driver starts the next skip when no IS_SKIPPING transaction is in flight, while the hero keeps acting.
- Each skip, in order: supported where it is → it stops ("stone_stops_skipping"); a Lily or Tree in its cell → it stops (on a Lily it rides, and kicks it); a Mirror one cell below → it stops skipping and sinks onto the Mirror, which gets a SMASH move ("smash") and becomes Rubble; else one cell along `move_theta` (beam powers apply) under the normal move check, where other skipping stones don't block. It wraps around the world.
- Supported means: no water at the cell, solid below, or a supporting entity below (another Rock, ...).
- If the next cell is blocked (a wall at its z, an entity standing there), it sinks in place. Unless something already supports it, it gets a one-cell fall step (0, 0, −1) through the power solver and the move check. If that passes, it flips (`properties` FLIPPING_TARGET, with a random flip axis when it has no `move_theta`) and its SKIPPING, CANNOT_MIRROR_TELEPORT_UNTIL_ANOTHER_SKIP_HAPPENS and SINK_IMMEDIATELY_... bits clear. From then on it is a plain unsupported Rock and falls as in [Falling](#falling), stopping at surface − 1 whatever the depth, so its top is a floor, also over deep columns.
- Kicks: the stone kicks the Lilies beside it ([Lilies](#lilies)) once per hop that moves it, and at the hop where it sinks in place when that one-cell fall passes the move check. It kicks nothing when it is supported where it is, when a Lily or Tree is in its cell (a Lily there is kicked along the stone's heading instead: the land-on-Lily kick), when it sinks from a collision with another skipping stone, or when the fall step of the sink fails the move check.
- A skip off the grid still moves and counts `num_out_of_bounds_skips`; on the 10th the SKIP move gets DISAPPEAR_DUE_TO_LEAVING_THE_MAP and the stone leaves the map.
- When two skipping stones meet in a cell, they compare `time_started_skipping` (tie: Pid order). The later one gets `SINK_IMMEDIATELY_INSTEAD_OF_SKIPPING_DUE_TO_COLLISION_WITH_ANOTHER_SKIPPING_STONE` ("stone_skip_odd/even_parity_rebound").
- Transmuting a stone mid-skip (`TRANSMUTING_STONE_THAT_IS_ALSO_SKIPPING`) deletes its pending SKIP moves.
- A stone that comes to rest on a Lily kicks it ([Lilies](#lilies)). Stones never carry the hero.

## Goblins, dragons and PROTECTION

- A GOBLIN attacks the Guys and Monsters within distance 1.1: its own cell and the six neighbours, above and below included. Other Goblins and dragons are Monsters, so they are hit too; Rovers are gathered with them but not killed. Only a blocking Fence on the crossed edge (a floor Fence, for the cells above and below) blocks the attack, unless the Goblin stands in a SQUEEZE beam: the attack takes the move check's fence test from the Goblin's cell to the target's, so a Fence in the Goblin's cell stops it leaving through that edge and one in the target's cell stops it entering. A target the Fence spares stays on the 3.5 wake list. Walls, objects, closed Gates and other Monsters block nothing: the Goblin's own cell is a target, and a closed Gate or a Monster beside it shields nothing. A target stuck inside a wall is skipped, unless the Goblin stands in a THROUGH beam. A Guy with `pre_protected` becomes `protected` instead of dying; every other Guy and Monster hit dies.
- Only a Monster with the DRAGON constant flag breathes fire; a Friendly_Dragon has none. It breathes along its local +x axis rounded to a grid axis (±0.5): `theta_target` 0 E, 90 N, 180 W, −90 S. Dragons never move on their own.
- The fire trace (shared with the wizard line) steps one cell at a time at the dragon's z, wraps, has no length limit and runs over water and air. It stops at a solid voxel, a column outside the level ([Support](#support)), a frozen Freezer, a closed Gate, a Tree, a Sign, a Wardrobe door edge, its own dragon or start cell, or outside the grid z range [−102, 110]. It hits and ends in the cell of a Gem, Guy, Rock (MIRRORABLE too), Promesst_Light, Monster, Friendly_Dragon or Rover. A Mirror turns it; fire may pass any number of Mirrors but ends at the same Mirror twice. Fences don't stop it. DEAD entities and ones already marked DIE_BY_ROASTING / DIE_BY_GOBLIN_ATTACK are ignored.
- `emitted_fire_length` is the summed segment length. The ray kills the Guys and Monsters in the cell it ends in; a Guy with `pre_protected` gets `protected` (and its turn group) instead. Rocks, lights, Rovers and Friendly_Dragons end the ray and do not die. If a Gem is among what the ray hits in that cell, nothing there is killed.
- A dead dragon (`dead_t`, DEAD) and a transmuted one (a crystal now) breathe no fire. A dragon stuck in a wall breathes none.
- Waking: each turn the Monster pass takes the nearest Guy within 3.5 (after a path and water check), else the nearest Rover within 3.5 at the same z (within 0.1). A target makes the Monster ACTIVE ("go_state_active"); none makes it inactive ("goblin_idle" / "gargoyle_idle"). The 3.5 gather keeps absent Rovers and absent group Guys ([Existence groups](ENTITIES.md#existence-groups)); attacks use the 1.1 list, which skips them.
- A goblin turns toward a target it sees at its own z: it stays in its cell with a new facing. No goblin is known to move on its own. The turn is a stationary move that carries the new facing: what rides the goblin moves by the goblin's displacement, which is none, and keeps its own facing. So a Mirror carried by a goblin does not turn when the goblin turns.
- A goblin's attack takes the living Monsters and Guys within the 1.1 distance, then drops those outside its existence group and those the Fence and wall rules above spare. Mirrors and other entities are never attacked. A goblin attacks whether it is ACTIVE or not (Waking sets only its animation state), and a goblin stuck inside a wall attacks nothing unless it stands in a THROUGH beam. So a Monster pushed beside a Goblin dies in that turn's attack, a Goblin or dragon included, and its death can empty a monster Gate's room ([Gates, switches and buttons](#gates-switches-and-buttons)).
- `pre_protected` = the Guy has PROTECTION (16) in `skill_flags`, or stands within 1.1 of such a Guy (above and below included), or is in the turn group of a Guy that does. A turn group is every existing Guy with the same `turn_order_id`; a Guy with none counts under his own entity id, so the Guys that move as one hero are one group, wherever they stand.
- `protected` is set only when a goblin or gargoyle attack, or fire, hits a `pre_protected` Guy, before anything in that pass dies. It goes onto the victim's whole turn group, and onto the turn group of every PROTECTION Guy within 1.1 of the victim, whether or not those Guys stand near the hit or are `pre_protected` themselves; a Guy beside the victim in another turn group gets nothing from the hit. It clears each protected Guy's `supported_by`.
- The protection pass runs in turn processing, after the move is enacted ([Falling](#falling)). Its first run in a turn copies `protected` to `was_protected`; every run clears `pre_protected` and `protected` on each Guy that has `pre_protected` when the pass starts, then recomputes `pre_protected`. A Guy who is `protected` without `pre_protected` at that moment (a member of a hit Guy's turn group who stands away from every PROTECTION Guy) keeps `protected` until a later pass finds him `pre_protected`, so he is held over water or air with nothing hitting him. A move reads `protected` as the previous turn left it. Runtime fields, not saved.
- A protected Guy is held where it stands, over water or air. In water that is the surface; once `protected` clears it falls to surface − 1 and drowns, so stepping out of fire into water is no route.
- A dry step into an attacked cell is never refused. The hero steps in, and the attack resolves in turn processing, from the positions after the move, against the recomputed `pre_protected`. So a hero who steps beside a PROTECTION Guy is safe, and a goblin that moved this turn attacks from its new cell. The only attack test in the move check is the water step's ([Water](#water)).
- Goblin attacks and fire are gathered in an attack pass, which runs twice in each settle step ([Falling](#falling)). Only the first pass kills; the second only sets `protected` on the `pre_protected` Guys it hits. A kill is a move of its own and gets its own settle step, so once a dragon's first victim is dead its fire reaches the next Monster in line in that settle's first pass: by the code, one dragon kills every Monster along its line in one input, one per settle step (unconfirmed in play). The fire trace ignores DEAD entities and ones already marked to die. All the rays of a pass are traced before any death, so two dragons whose fire lines meet kill each other.
- Rocks, Mirrors and other objects are never in an attack's targets, so a cell holding a Rock is not attacked. A hero who steps into a goblin's neighbour cell as its Rock leaves is hit, because he stands within range at the next attack pass.

## Promesst beams and powers

- A `Promesst_Light` casts its beam while `powered`. Its room is powered when the room has at least one counted Gem_Slot and every counted slot is filled. A slot counts only while it exists. It is filled by a free Gem in its cell (`attached_to_entity_id` 0), or when its `flags` hold BEAM_POWERED (a GEM beam, below). Slot power, and then each light's `powered`, is recomputed when a Group's existence changes, at load and during turn processing: every update resets every light's `powered` from its room, whatever was stored. A Group change recounts the rooms whenever its changed members include a Gem_Slot or a Gem, so a Group that makes an unfilled slot exist turns its room's lights off. So a light in a room with a filled slot is on even where the level data stores `powered` false. The save stores `powered` changes (a 1-byte bool). A light in a frozen Freezer's cell casts no beam.
- A room is a 19 × 13 screen in the ground, underworld and sky layers, and a whole map-room layer above them ([Screens and rooms](WORLD.md#screens-and-rooms)). So one filled Gem_Slot in a map room powers every light in that map room.
- Magic Mirrors use no room power ([Magic mirrors](#magic-mirrors)).
- Direction: the light's local +x axis rounded to a grid axis, or straight up / down with `vertical_up` / `vertical_down`.
- The beam steps one cell at a time, wraps, and passes solid voxels, Guys, Monsters, MIRRORABLE Rocks and `mirrorable` Rovers (colouring them). It stops at any Promesst_Light cell (so one lap at most, ending at its own light), a non-MIRRORABLE Rock, a non-mirrorable Rover, a Friendly_Dragon, Tree, Sign, Treasure_Display, Gem, Egg, a closed Gate (a pending GATE_OPEN or GATE_CLOSE counts), a frozen Freezer, the grid edge or z range. `stalled` and Rover activity are not read. A Mirror turns it ([Mirrors turning fire and beams](#mirrors-turning-fire-and-beams)); the beam traces itself again from each Mirror, into the same sample list.
- Solid voxels never stop a beam: the cell lookup reads entities only. So a vertical beam leaves its layer. On the overworld a beam segment that starts below the map rooms ends at the bottom of the lowest FAST_TRAVEL box − 0.5, about z 74.66: a beam turned up at z 0 climbs to z 74 unless an entity stops it.
- A beam is at infinity (`endpoint_b_is_at_infinity`) when its next sample would leave the level volume: outside the grid in x or y (never on a wrapping level, which wraps first), below `grid.corner.z`, above `grid.corner.z` + `squares_z`, or past the map-room cap above. The flag is reset for every light on every beam update and describes the path's last segment; `endpoint_b` is then the last cell inside the volume. On the overworld only a vertical beam can be at infinity.
- Beam powers act on whatever stands in the beam (`Colors_Struct.active_counts` per colour): each beam adds 1 to its colour's count on every Guy, MIRRORABLE Rock, Mirror, Rover and Monster it passes. Only those types carry counts; a Rock, Mirror or Rover uses them only when MIRRORABLE / `mirrorable`. Each light has one `color`, so ALTER comes from its own beam. The beam update retraces all beams (clearing every Guy's Colors_Struct) in every settle step (the turn order under [Falling](#falling)), after the protection pass and the switch update, and once more after a move's position write when a beam carrier moved.

| Colour | Effect |
|---|---|
| THROUGH | the mover may enter a solid cell, and one inside a wall may leave it. The move check skips the wall test for a mover in a THROUGH beam |
| ENDPOINT | a move parallel to the beam where it hits the mover (\|dot(move, `beam_direction_when_hitting_object`)\| ≥ 0.5): along the beam jumps to `endpoint_b`, against it to `endpoint_a`. `endpoint_a` is the first sample after the light; `endpoint_b` the last in-range sample of the whole path, after Mirror turns too. So a hero on a leg that a Mirror turns up jumps to the top of the vertical leg. A fall step goes through the power solver like any move, so it is an ENDPOINT jump too whenever the fall (0, 0, −1) is parallel to the beam where it hits the faller: a `vertical_up` light, or any vertical leg that a Mirror turned up. Against an upward leg the fall becomes a jump to `endpoint_a`, not a drop. The jump is checked with the same move check as every TELEPORT and ENDPOINT jump (landing cell only: solid voxel, blocking occupant). If the landing is refused, the fall just ends where he stands: no fall, no support needed, no further step down. A beam passes solid voxels and Mirrors, so `endpoint_a` is often a wall cell or a Mirror's cell, and then a hero who steps off a ledge into the leg hangs at his height and walks on from it. If the landing passes, the jump comes in two stages: the fall first gets a hold (a STATIONARY move), and the turn's post-move processing then adds the jump itself (TELEPORT, "character_beam_use_endpoint_vertical"). With ALTER ("Midpoint beam"): halfway, towards = idx + ⌈(n−1−idx)/2⌉, away = ⌊idx/2⌋ over the light's samples. A move along a beam at infinity is different, with or without ALTER: the mover jumps to `endpoint_b` and leaves the map (a TELEPORT with DISAPPEAR_AFTER_TELEPORT, DISAPPEAR_DUE_TO_LEAVING_THE_MAP and TELEPORTING_FORWARD). When it finishes, a Guy (not PLAYABLE_IN_PRELUDE) first breaks the level's Doors ("door_break_hard": every Door not PLAYABLE_IN_PRELUDE whose `enabled_state` is ENABLED becomes BROKEN_HARD, the level gets AUDIO_MUFFLED_MODE and "level_fail_mode_begin" plays), then the mover is destroyed. Only undo brings him back. That happens for any finished move of a Guy flagged DISAPPEAR_DUE_TO_LEAVING_THE_MAP, not only this jump. A broken exit Door makes the level unwinnable ([Winning and other inputs](SKILLS.md#winning-and-other-inputs)). The soft break ("door_break_soft", BROKEN_SOFT) runs from the kill and from the fall step, and from one more place ([Out of scope](UNCONFIRMED.md#out-of-scope)). So a vertical leg that nothing stops is a death, not a lift. In an ENDPOINT jump the wizard swaps with whatever stands at the endpoint |
| DOUBLE | every move ×2 per DOUBLE beam, ×3 per beam with ALTER, any direction. Only the destination is wall-checked; fences only in the start and destination cells |
| SQUEEZE | the mover passes Fences. SQUEEZE + ALTER bends the bars ("bend_bars"): crossing a Fence sets its `blocking` off and swaps in the bent mesh, so it stops blocking everyone. The Fence is marked changed for the turn's undo record. `blocking` and the base `mesh` are both saved fields, and undo and load apply them like any field (the mesh is re-set when the stored one differs from the drawn one), so undo puts the bars back |
| STRONG | +1 push strength per beam ([Pushing and pulling](#pushing-and-pulling)). STRONG + ALTER cancels pushing and gives PULL |
| SMASH | without ALTER, two ways. A step that pushes smashes only when the push fails: the bump over the push list ([Pushing and pulling](#pushing-and-pulling)) takes entries by the hero's push strength (none: the first live entry; 1: the first two; 2 or more: all; on a weight refusal the entries past the allowed count lose their pusher first). Each such entry whose pusher (the hero, or the thing behind it in the row) has SMASH and no ALTER gets a SMASH move if it is a Rock or Rover, unless a frozen Freezer shares its cell; a Gate entry gets the OPEN bump instead. A successful push smashes nothing. The move check's own SMASH test runs only in move checks with no push list or with pushing off: an ENDPOINT jump that lands on a Rock or Rover, the charm move ([CHARM](SKILLS.md#charm)), a fall onto it, elevator and other scripted moves. It doesn't smash through a Fence on the edge of a 1-cell move (unless SQUEEZE; longer moves ignore Fences) or into a frozen Freezer's cell. The target gets a SMASH (21) move, so the mover's own move fails (a bump, or STATIONARY after a jump); at turn processing it becomes Rubble (`rubble_type` its Rubble kind, below; `source_entity_id`) |
| OPEN | moving into a closed Gate opens it; with ALTER, moving into an open Gate shuts it ([Gates, switches and buttons](#gates-switches-and-buttons)) |
| GEM | powers Gem_Slots near its carriers. For a Guy, Rock, Monster or Mirror in a GEM beam and in no ALTER beam, every Gem_Slot within 1.5 (wrap-aware) is gathered; with ALTER as well, every Gem_Slot in the carrier's screen room. A gathered slot gets `flags` BEAM_POWERED ("promesst_slot_powered"); one that had it and was not gathered loses it ("promesst_slot_unpowered"). A Rover in a GEM beam powers no slot. Neither the carriers nor the slots are tested for existence. The gather runs with the turn's beam update, from beams cast with each light's `powered` as it stood before the gather. It changes only slot flags, so a second pass finds the same slots and ends the loop. If a slot changed (it gained BEAM_POWERED, or lost it with no free Gem in its cell), slot power and light power are recounted and the beams cast again, with no further gather. So a chain of rooms linked by GEM beams advances one room per beam update: a light that the recount turns on or off changes the slots its GEM or ALTER beam reaches only at the next beam update, and level load settles two steps. On the overworld the gather runs only while the hero stands in the underworld layer; elsewhere BEAM_POWERED keeps its last value and still counts as filled. The overworld's GEM lights are all in the underworld |
| ALTER | a colour of its own; alone it does nothing. It changes the other colours on the same entity: DOUBLE ×3, ENDPOINT to the midpoint, SQUEEZE bends bars, STRONG becomes PULL, SMASH smashes a whole kind (below), OPEN as listed, GEM gathers the whole screen room |

- SMASH + ALTER ("multismash"): the ordinary SMASH rules need ALTER at 0, so this is a rule of its own, run in turn processing. Every live coloured entity in both SMASH and ALTER that stands on Rubble smashes that Rubble (it is removed) and gives a SMASH move to every entity in the same screen room whose Rubble kind is that Rubble's `rubble_type`. So a hero in SMASH + ALTER who steps onto ROCK Rubble turns every plain Rock of the room to Rubble.
- Rubble kinds (`Rubble_Type`): a Rock is ROCK, CRYSTAL if MIRRORABLE, STONE_SKIPPABLE / STONE_SKIPPABLE_CRYSTAL if SKIPPABLE, none with NO_RUBBLE. A plain Mirror is MIRROR_PLAIN; a magic one MIRROR_MIRRORABLE if `mirrorable`, else MIRROR_MAGIC. A Rover is ROVER, a Promesst_Light PROMESST_LIGHT, a Gem GEM.
- DOUBLE and falling: a fall step is (0, 0, −1) through the same power solver, so in a DOUBLE beam it is 2 cells. The support check looks 2 down: a solid 2 below holds the Guy even with air between, a one-cell floor with air under it does not. A Lily in the cell cancels the doubling. A Guy at the surface in a DOUBLE beam has a fall target of surface − 2, inside the water landing window, so the step is cancelled: he stands on the water, outside the drown window. A Guy stepping sideways into a DOUBLE beam over water is already in the beam when his fall is checked, so he stands on the water too. On the beam he hops sample to sample over water, or 2 cells sideways across a 1-wide gap. With ALTER + DOUBLE (×3) the fall target is surface − 3. The step is cancelled only when surface − 3 < target z < surface − 1, so it is not cancelled, and goes ahead if that cell passes the move check. The water lookup finds only maps with z ≤ pos.z + 2.1, so at surface − 3 the water is out of reach and the hero counts as dry. Where the dry implicit floor holds (ground and map rooms, z ≤ the layer base) he stands there, 3 under the surface, and does not drown: the drown window ends 2.5 under. Elsewhere he falls on to the first support. With something solid 3 below, he stays on the surface.
- Beam-blocker Rocks are named `*beam_blocker` / `*beam_barrier*`.

## Mirrors turning fire and beams

- Every Mirror turns fire and beams, magic or not; `is_magic` and `mirrorable` don't matter. Facing = `theta_target` (degrees about z).
- A flat Mirror has two open sides, R(θ)·(−1, 0) and R(θ)·(0, −1): west and south at θ = 0. A ray entering through one open side leaves through the other. Output directions are rounded per axis to −1/0/+1.
- `is_vertical` without `is_vertical_downward`: open sides R(θ)·(+1, 0) and up. At θ 0 a ray moving west turns up and a ray moving down turns east; at θ 180 a ray moving east turns up, a ray moving down turns west and a ray moving west hits the back. With `is_vertical_downward`: R(θ)·(−1, 0) and down. At θ 0 a ray moving east turns down and a ray moving up turns west; at θ 90 the open sides are south and down, so a rising ray turns south.
- After a turn the trace starts again from the Mirror's cell. It keeps one sample list and one pair of endpoints for the whole path.
- The back or a closed side ends the ray: fire counts it as a hit; a beam is blocked, and a `mirrorable` Mirror is coloured. DEAD Mirrors are ignored.

## Magic mirrors

- A magic Mirror's arms are its two open sides, which come from its facing as for any Mirror ([Mirrors turning fire and beams](#mirrors-turning-fire-and-beams)): −x and −y turned by `theta_target`. It casts rays along both arms, up to max(W, H, 100) cells, and keeps the nearer target. Targets: Guys, Monsters, MIRRORABLE Rocks (not one with both SKIPPING and CANNOT_MIRROR_TELEPORT_UNTIL_ANOTHER_SKIP_HAPPENS), `mirrorable` Rovers, `mirrorable` Mirrors. The ray ends at a cell holding a Sign, Tree, Gem, Promesst_Light, a Rover or Mirror that is not `mirrorable`, a non-MIRRORABLE Rock, a closed Gate or a frozen Freezer. The ray stops at a Gate whose `open` is false when the ray is cast, the same test as the shared line tracer; a pending GATE_OPEN or GATE_CLOSE is not looked at. An open Gate does not stop it.
- The mirage (`Mirage.target_position`) stands as far behind the Mirror along the reflected path as the target stands in front, found by tracing back through the Mirror for 2 × the distance, following reflections: from the target back to the Mirror (d cells), turned by the Mirror's open sides, then d cells out the other arm.
- Solid cells are found with the level's voxel solid query, the same one the move check and the support check use ([Support](#support)). The mirage update applies it twice: the ray stops at the first solid voxel, on the target arm and on the landing leg alike, and the landing cell is tested again on its own. There is no THROUGH exception, since the update re-tests the voxel itself, so a landing inside a wall is never accepted.
- A mirage is PRIMARY_BLOCKER (`valid_state`, 2 for a solid landing; `went_beyond_the_blocker`), and ACTIVATE gives "cannot_mirror", when the reflection is ambiguous (two different targets at equal distance), the landing leg ends early at a solid voxel, the trace crosses a refusing edge (below), the landing cell is solid or holds a Gem, or the reflected move (flagged MOVED_BY_MIRROR) fails the teleport move check. Every other mirage in the rooms of the heroes whose turn it is then becomes SECONDARY_BLOCKED. A mirage can't push, so that check refuses any occupant of the landing cell that blocks a move: any Rock (MIRRORABLE or not), Mirror, Guy, Monster, Rover, Tree or frozen Freezer. A floor Fence in the landing cell is accepted: Fence occupants pass, and the floor test only blocks a move with a vertical component.
- The teleport itself is an ordinary move (strings "MirrorLeave", "MirrorArrive", "teleporting_mirror"). When the moves finish, the ordinary support check runs on the landing, so an unsupported landing falls ([Falling](#falling)), and then the drown check: a Guy or Monster that lands 1 or 2 cells under a water surface drowns at once, and one that lands at the surface or above it starts a fall and drowns when the fall step finishes. The only special case is a skipping Rock landing in water (below).
- Refusing edges: a Wardrobe door edge, anywhere, in a level with Wardrobes; and a blocking Fence with `is_solid_wall` on the crossed edge (`is_floor` for a vertical step), tested only when the ray starts inside a FAST_TRAVEL `Camera_Marker` box, so only in the map rooms. Other Fences never refuse.
- A mirage is processed only while the Mirror's room holds a Guy of the active hero. In a map room that room is the whole map-room layer. Magic Mirrors don't read room power, so a magic Mirror works in an unpowered room.
- ACTIVATE teleports all reflections at once only if every active mirage is OK. Otherwise nothing moves ("cannot_mirror"). All or nothing.
- A magic Mirror whose rays find no target adds no mirage, so it spoils no activation of another magic Mirror in its room. A hero on a ray of that second Mirror is its target as well.
- Two OK mirages with `target_position`s within 0.5 (wrap-aware) cancel the activation unless they match: the same reflected entity in two mirrors is a CLONE (it appears at both); two matching entities on one target are CLONE_UNIFY (they merge after the teleport). Two Rocks match when their MIRRORABLE and SKIPPABLE bits agree. Others need the same type and `mesh`; two Guys must also be the same hero (same id or a shared `turn_order_id` source) carrying the same number of Gems ("non_matching_gems" otherwise; the Gems flash).
- Clones and Pids: Mirrors are processed in the level's Mirror list order, by index from the first. The first Mirror whose mirage reflects an entity moves the original, which keeps its Pid. Each later one first copies it (a new runtime Pid, its mirage retargeted to the copy), copies the Gems it carries and attaches them to the copy.
- The Mirror list is one of the per-type entity lists. Registering an entity (load, creation, a copy, undo re-creating it) appends it at the end. Destroying one (smashed to Rubble, merged by a clone unify, transmuted, leaving the map) removes it at once by moving the last entry into its place. So after a Mirror goes, the Mirror that was last is processed earlier, and a Mirror added at run time, a copy of a cloned `mirrorable` Mirror too, is processed last.
- Facing: the teleport stores a quarter-turn facing on each TELEPORT move. A flat Mirror's facing is its bisector (θ − 135°, between its open sides) put through the mirage's transform, plus 135°, so its open sides go to the images of its old open sides. A vertical Mirror's `theta_target` is passed through unchanged. Every other type's θ is put through the transform, wrapped. When any TELEPORT move finishes, `theta_target` is set to 90 × q for the stored quarter turn q (q = 3 gives −90), and a Mirror's sides are read from `theta_target`. So a reflected Guy or Monster faces the reflected way, a teleported flat Mirror turns unless the transform maps its bisector to itself, and a vertical Mirror keeps its facing.
- The activation can teleport a skipping stone. A stone that lands unsupported in water gets CANNOT_MIRROR_TELEPORT_UNTIL_ANOTHER_SKIP_HAPPENS. A SKIPPABLE Rock moved by a teleport takes its new `move_theta` from the teleport move's facing, which the mirror reflects, so it skips on in the reflected direction:
  - The stone's `move_theta` is rounded to a grid axis and transformed by the mirage's transform, as every facing is. The result is stored as a cardinal Direction on its TELEPORT move. After the move a SKIPPABLE Rock gets `move_theta` = 90° × (Direction − 1), wrapped to (−180, 180]: EAST 0, NORTH 90, WEST −180, SOUTH −90 (−1 if not cardinal).
  - The first Mirror on the path gives the reflection that swaps its open sides a = R(θ)·(−1, 0) and b = R(θ)·(0, −1), for any θ: heading in toward the Mirror (−a) becomes −b, heading out (+a) becomes +b, and ±b becomes ±a.
  - Each further Mirror adds a quarter turn that does not depend on any Mirror's θ: −90° (+x → −y) when the ray enters it through its side a and leaves through b, +90° when it enters through b. That is the turn the ray itself makes at that Mirror. The stone's grid-rounded heading gets Mirror 1's reflection, then the turns of Mirrors 2 … n.
  - A FALLING stone that is not SKIPPING uses the image of straight down instead. If that image is horizontal (a vertical Mirror), the move gets a different teleport kind.
- A skipping stone passing over a Mirror smashes it instead ([Skipping stones](#skipping-stones)).

## Rovers

- A Rover shuttles one square at a time along its facing axis and flips `forward` at an obstacle. Each step saves `base.position`, `visual_position` and `forward`.
- The level has one rover timer. It starts at `Settings.time_per_rover_update`: the type default is 0.6 s, the overworld has 1.0 s, a level with no Settings entity uses 0.25 s. It runs down in real time, not turns, and only while the level has Rovers. At 0 every Rover steps at once (IS_ROVER_TRANSACTION), wherever the hero is, and the period is added back. On the overworld that is about every 7 hero moves at a steady walk. Every undo record stores the timer; undo and load restore it from the newest record.
- Only some overworld Rovers move, each on a straight track of a few squares, most in the sky and the underworld. The others are stuck.
- A Rover that meets an obstacle reverses along the same axis: it drives back the way it came (180°), never a quarter turn. The axis is its `theta_target`: an even quarter turn runs it east-west, an odd one north-south.
- A Rover pushed into water sinks to surface − 1 like any pushed object ([Objects in water](#objects-in-water)) and keeps shuttling along its axis under the water, one square per step, reversing at a solid voxel or an object in the next square. It carries the hero standing on it (his cell is at the surface, the Rover under him) along the track, and he can step off onto any ground that borders the track. A push sets `stalled` and `was_stalled`; they clear on the next two steps, and the Rover runs on with `forward` flipped.
- What stands on a Rover rides along (`supported_by_id` = the Rover; RIDE_ON_ROVER). One MINOR pad in the underworld rides a Rover along its track. A Rover pushes what is in front of it (`StateBeingPushedRover`). The hero can push a Rover.

## Lilies

- Lilies drift in real time, one step at a time, one move transaction per `movement_group` that renews itself when it finishes. They keep drifting while the hero stands still. Groups: `hs_ika`, `lower_falls`, `mixup`, `wizard_nw`, `upper_falls`; `nonmoving` in the map rooms. A step is a LILY (19) move.
- Step timing: a LILY move, and the STATIONARY moves made with it, lasts 2 × `Settings.time_per_lily_update` (type default 0.4 s, so 0.8 s). The lily's position is written halfway, `time_per_lily_update` after the start. When a group's transaction finishes, the frame update renews its move at once, so the group steps every 2 × `time_per_lily_update`. Lily transactions never block the player ([A move](#a-move)).
- Direction per step: `kick_direction` if kicked; else the flow under the lily, when water is there and the lily is at most 0.5 above the surface; else its last `moving_direction` (it coasts across still water). Lilies within 0.1 of a rewritten river square take the new direction. `moving_direction` never returns to NONE in the saves.
- A lily's step is blocked by: no water at its level with something solid under the target, a solid voxel, a Rock, Judge, Promesst_Light, Gem, Rover, Tree, Sign or Treasure_Display, a closed Gate or frozen Freezer, a Mirror, Monster or Friendly_Dragon, another lily (sounds `lily_bumps_against_terrain` / `object` / `lily`). An entity in the target cell whose supporter chain ends at a Lily is skipped, so a rider never blocks its own lily. A protected Guy in the target is skipped. With no water at the lily's level and nothing solid under the target, the lily moves off the edge and falls.
- Riding: the support check counts a Lily in an entity's own cell as its support, so a hero or rock boards by walking or falling into the lily's cell. When a move transaction closes, every entity supported by a mover gets the mover's delta as a SUPPORT (9) move with the mover's duration, riders of riders too, for elevator, charmed, pulled, pushed, interpolated, support, controller, lily and skip moves, and for any move that turns except a teleport. A fall carries its riders by starting their own falls ([Falling](#falling)). A rider whose own move fails gets STATIONARY + UPDATE_SUPPORT_DESPITE_STATIONARY + SUPPORT_WILL_BE_LOST: it stays and loses its support. Riders copy DISAPPEAR_DUE_TO_LEAVING_THE_MAP and leave the map with the lily.
- Stepping off: input is never gated while riding. The hero's step starts from his current cell and goes through the normal move check. If his supporter has a real move in another in-flight transaction, the hero's transaction gets that transaction's physical-move time minus 0.01 s, so his step resolves just before the lily's. When it closes, his SUPPORT move is dropped from the other transactions and the lily drifts on without him.
- Kicks come only from skipping stones: a SKIPPING stone that comes to rest supported by a Lily kicks it along the stone's `move_theta` (`land_on_lily`); a stone skipping over water pushes the lilies beside it outward (±90° of its heading, ±135° on a diagonal): at each hop ([Skipping stones](#skipping-stones)), a lily in a cell next to the stone's cell, on either side of the heading, is kicked away from the stone's lane. A lily on the lane is not pushed this way: it stops the stone. A kick on a lily mid-step re-targets it at once (riders recomputed; a blocked kick leaves it still); otherwise it sets `kick_direction`, and a kick opposite the pending one cancels it. The next step takes the kick once `Settings.lily_kickoff_time` (0.4 s when negative) has passed since `Lily.kickoff_delay_start_time`, then clears it. The hero never kicks a lily.
- DIAGONAL_LILY_PUSH (`Move_Info.flags` 0x4000): on a level with a `diagonal_push_okay` Marker (only `skipping_pals`), a hero riding a Lily who steps toward an oncoming Lily whose rider stands in the marker can push that rider; it animates diagonally. Visual only. Without the marker the step is blocked.
- Stepping toward an oncoming Lily: when the hero stands on a Lily drifting in a Lily transaction and steps toward an oncoming Lily (its step is the opposite of his Lily's and it arrives at his target), whatever rides that Lily is given the hero's step from its riding position and put through the move check without pushes. So SMASH can apply to it.
- `RIDE_ON_LILY` is `Action_Icon` 20 (a history icon), not a move type.

## Rivers

- A river is the flow `Direction` in water squares ([Water](#water)). Several overworld maps hold rivers. The level data has no `Flow_Updater`; a Flow_Updater is a short-lived front created when a river is blocked or freed.
- A river square is blocked when one of these settles in it with its top at the surface (entity z = surface − 1): Rock, Judge, Promesst_Light, Gem, Rover, Tree, Sign, Treasure_Display, Mirror, a closed Gate or frozen Freezer. Guys, Monsters, Lilies and Eggs never block. Deeper sunk rocks don't block. GATE_CLOSE / GATE_OPEN block and free.
- Blocking spawns: an `ERASING` front walking downstream setting direction 0 (the water stays), stopping where another current still feeds the square, at a block or at a `stop_flow_erasure` Marker; and for each upstream neighbour that flowed in, a `DIVERTING_RIGHT` / `DIVERTING_LEFT` front by its `river_polarity` (TURNS_RIGHT → right). It turns the river 90° that way if that side has water at the surface and no blocker and does not flow back; else the other side; else `DIVERTING_GIVE_UP`. Once turned, the polarity becomes the side taken and the front continues as `FLOWING_FORWARD`, writing its direction into still water until it meets flowing water (merge), no water, or a block (it diverts again). A flat Mirror blocker (not `is_vertical`) picks the side itself when the flow enters through one of its open sides (flow direction · that side < −0.5): the front turns toward the other open side, DIVERTING_LEFT if that side lies along the flow turned 90° left (dot > 0.8), DIVERTING_RIGHT if it lies opposite. A flow that hits its back, and a vertical Mirror, use the polarity rule.
- Freeing a square lets the upstream current back in and a FLOWING_FORWARD front refills the erased channel. Diverted squares keep their new flow, so the result depends on history.
- Every write is an OCEAN_CHANGE {pid, index, old, new}. A Flow_Updater only rewrites squares that already hold water: `ocean_values[idx] = 1 | (river_polarity == 1) << 1 | direction << 2`.

## Elevators

- An elevator is a `Stairs` frame pair (`other_end_id`, `platform_id`) and one `Elevator_Platform` (`bottom_owner`, `top_owner`, `seconds_per_square`). No direction field: no elevator is one-way by design. The platform's state is its position; in the level data most rest at the bottom, the rest at the top. A few are existence-group content.
- The rider stands in the platform's cell. A Guy moving into the platform's cell starts it. The frame does not trigger it. It moves one square per step as a normal move (it never enters a solid voxel), re-checked each step, until within 0.5 of the far frame. The lower half of the shaft goes up, the upper half down; it goes down unless a blocker is directly below.
- Up: refused with `elevator_too_heavy` when the stack riding it counts 2 or more. It is the push weight's count ([Pushing and pulling](#pushing-and-pulling)): living Guys and Monsters, Friendly_Dragons and every other entity except Switch, Lily, Artifact, Door, Gate, Rubble, Flat_Decoration, Level_Entry, Elevator_Platform, Gem_Slot, Teleporter, FLAT entities and unfrozen Freezers; a following Friendly_Dragon ends it. The hero counts, so he rides up alone and never with a Rock. Also refused by a non-following Friendly_Dragon directly above or by the move check.
- Down: no weight limit. A step is refused (`promesst_elevator_end_blocked`) by a Rock, Judge, Promesst_Light, Gem, Rover, Tree, Sign, Treasure_Display, closed Gate, frozen Freezer, Guy, Monster or non-following Friendly_Dragon in the next cell. A Mirror there is smashed.
- Calling: moving into a `Stairs` cell whose platform is idle and more than 0.1 away calls it there under the same blocking rules; the hero doesn't move. A blocking non-floor Fence on that edge stops the call. The calling path has no weight check: it runs the platform steps directly, and the weight count is only used by boarding, the push-weight sum and the weight crush. The hero isn't on the platform when he calls it, so a platform can be called up with a Rock on it.
- A platform is never stuck: the stuck test exempts Elevator_Platforms, and a step tests only the next cell. So a platform resting in a frozen Freezer's cell leaves it when called up, but can't come back down into it: the frozen Freezer refuses the last down step. The hero can't enter that cell either, so such an elevator links nothing while the Freezer is closed. Some overworld elevators have their platform in the frozen Freezer of their bottom frame cell.
- Per elevator (level data): one links nothing (a crystal stands on its platform in a cage fenced on four sides); the map-room copy of the hub elevator works bottom → top only (a Rock stands on it: call it down, push the rock off, ride up alone; `SmashFastTravelRock` also destroys that rock); the hub elevator, between the underworld and the sky, clears only from the top ([Routes that need these mechanics](#routes-that-need-these-mechanics)); those in frozen Freezers are blocked while their Freezer is closed; the rest work both ways.

## Teleporter pads and fast travel

- A pad sends a controlled Guy who stands on it to its `target_id` when `enabled_t_target` ≥ 0.5. The pad check runs only at the end of a processed hero input, after the move resolves (inferred from the call order), and it and the pad's visual scan run only over the controlled Guys. A pushed Mirror, Rock or Promesst_Light on an enabled pad stays where it is.
- A fall never fires a pad. Falls are their own falling transactions, and neither a fall step nor a landing runs the pad check. The check has no arrival condition: it looks only at where each controlled Guy stands. So a hero who falls onto a pad stays there until the end of his next input; it fires then if he still stands on it (a bump, or ACTIVATE), and not if the input took him off. Stepping back onto it later fires it.
- The target is looked up in the whole entity table with no type check; the hero goes to the target's position (wrapped). The pad builds a TELEPORT move there and adds it only if the move check passes, so a target cell holding a Tree, Sign, Judge, Treasure_Display or frozen Freezer keeps the hero on the pad.
- A world MAJOR pad's target is its twin in a map room, and the twin's target is the world pad. Fast travel is: step on a pad, walk in the map room to an enabled twin, step on it. There is no direct world-to-world jump.
- Many MINOR pads target a Marker, not a pad, mostly in the sky and the underworld; the hero lands at the Marker and falls from there. A Marker target may stand far above the pad, so such a pad can lift the hero.
- Stepping on a pad discovers it. `discover(T)` does nothing when `enabled_t_target` = 0. Else it sets `discovered`, then: `target_id` is enabled and discovered with this check; `also_discover` is enabled, its target enabled, then discovered with chaining; `also_discover2` is enabled and set `discovered` without chaining. A cycle guard reports "Infinite loop detected in teleporter also_discover chain".
- `enable(T)` sets `enabled_t_target` = 1. For a MAJOR pad it also makes the pad's `group_id` Group exist when that Group `is_existence_group` and does not exist, and the same for `also_exist_this_group_id`. Callers: the discovery chain, Tower activation, the discovered-teleporter carry-over.
- Every `also_discover` link sits on a map-room twin and names another twin. Discovering a twin enables the twins it names and their world pads and makes their Groups exist.
- The MAJOR pads of the sky antipode hub (the sky towers' pads, [Second Endgame](PROGRESSION.md#second-endgame)) point `also_exist_this_group_id` at Group `FTEnd2` (FAST_TRAVEL_SKY, `exists_by_default` 0, in the sky map room). They are the targets of the sky map room's twins.
- Fast-travel Markers act on touch (on the overworld, all in the world, none in the map rooms): `FastTravelEnableGroup` makes its target Group exist (those at the hub enable `FTEnd1`; the one in the ruby lift's landing cell enables `FastTravelVerticalAccess`, the map room's copy of the lift, so the copy turns on after the first ride, [Routes that need these mechanics](#routes-that-need-these-mechanics)); `FastTravelDiscoverTeleporter` discovers its target pad with the check (one, in the same landing cell); `SmashFastTravelRock`, on the hub elevator's top cell, when the hero stands still on it, destroys `ruby_sky_fast_travel_elevator_rock`, the rock on the map-room copy of that elevator, not the playable stack. That makes the map-room elevator usable.
- Pads, the wizard swap and the hero swap never write `skill_flags`.
- Gold rooms: the ground Towers' `teleporter_id` are MINOR pads (disabled) next to each tower. Their targets are the `gold_room_endpoint` pads in the ground antipode hub, one per tower, half a world period from the crossroads ([Endgame](PROGRESSION.md#endgame)). Tower activation enables the pad.

## Activators and Spawners

- The hero's use/interact action (ACTIVATE) finds an Activator in his own cell and runs each of its Spawners (`overworld_teleport_and_reset`, `object_respawned`). A Spawner destroys the entities whose `transmute_spawner_source_id` points at it and the entity holding `spawned_entity_id`, then clones `prototype_entity_to_clone` with the id `spawned_entity_id`. The Pid is reused. In the save a reset is one record holding a CREATION_OR_DESTRUCTION destroy then a create for the same Pid, in that order.
- An Ocean_Map Spawner (`ocean_resetter` set) copies its prototype's `ocean_values` into the live map, logs one OCEAN_CHANGE per changed square (old = live, new = prototype) and kills that map's Flow_Updaters. That can clear bit 0: water can drain again.
- Respawns happen only on ACTIVATE. A level load binds each Spawner to at most two Activators and builds its prototype, but spawns nothing.
- A Spawner's clone appears at the Spawner's own cell. The old instance is destroyed first and the clone reuses its id, so there is one instance at a time. The respawn is skipped while the Spawner's cell is blocked, and the old instance then stays. The clone keeps the prototype's movement group. The Spawner sets none of the Lily's direction fields (`kick_direction`, `moving_direction`) when it makes the clone. The clone's first step is chosen by the ordinary rule ([Lilies](#lilies)): `kick_direction`, else the flow under the Lily when it lies at most 0.5 above the surface, else the prototype's `moving_direction` (NONE in the level data). So a clone on a river first drifts with the flow under the Spawner's cell, and a clone on still water does not move.
- A Lily Spawner usually stands in the Lily's own cell; seven stand along one underworld river row, each beside its own Activator, for one Lily, so the Lily comes back where the hero calls it.
- A Rock's Spawner almost always stands in the Rock's own starting cell, so a reset puts a Rock back where it started, wherever the hero pushed it, and also takes a Rock that sank in water away. The sunk Rock is the bridge ([Objects in water](#objects-in-water)); the water square itself is not changed (no OCEAN_CHANGE), so the reset takes the bridge back: the water cell is open again, nothing stays of the sunk floor, and the new Rock stands at the Spawner's cell, where it can be pushed in again. A hero standing on the bridge then falls into the water (inferred, [Support](#support)).
- The overworld's ocean resets are on the ground and in the underworld: each Activator stands near its map and also respawns the puzzle's pieces, a few Lilies and Rocks, in one a Promesst_Light as well and in one a Mirror.
