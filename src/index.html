Order of the Sinking Star Overworld Map and Save Viewer

An unofficial, interactive overworld map and save viewer for Order of the Sinking Star. Open your save folder to see your progress on the map: which levels you have solved, which you can play now and what is left to find. Places you have not explored stay under fog, so the map is spoiler-free.

Track your progress

  • Pan and zoom around the overworld map, which wraps around just like in the game.
  • See the levels left for your next goal, with the ones you can play now listed first.
  • Follow your goals from the game's endings through to achievements and full completion.
  • In supported browsers the map updates by itself each time the game saves.

Where to find your save files

On Windows, Order of the Sinking Star keeps its save files in %USERPROFILE%\Saved Games\Order of the Sinking Star. On Linux with Proton, the same folder is inside the game's Steam compatibility data. Pick that folder or drag it onto the page.

Private by design

Your save is read locally in your browser and is never uploaded. The viewer is free and open source.
